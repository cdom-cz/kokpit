# Quick task 261030-u20: Supplier company name as e-mail sender name

Use the company name from the supplier settings as the display name of the From address of invoice, document and payment confirmation e-mails.

- Add `SupplierSender::address()` (configured from address + `SupplierSettings::company_name`).
- Use it in the envelope of `InvoiceMail` and `PaymentConfirmationMail`.
- Test: the payment confirmation is sent from the supplier company name.
