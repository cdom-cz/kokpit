Design notes for the executor:
- Domain Actions throw `ValidationException` with bare keys (`key`, `hourly_rate`, `client_id`, ...). The Filament pages wrap every Action call in `RethrowsDomainValidation::withFormErrors()` which rethrows the same messages with keys prefixed by `data.`, so errors land next to the right field.
- Tags: the plugin's tags input (type `project`) keeps its own relationship load and save after the record exists; the pages do not pass `tags` to the Actions.
- Archived-record access: `getEloquentQuery()` and `getRecordRouteBindingEloquentQuery()` remove only `SoftDeletingScope` (argument form, allowed by the escape-hatch test of plan 04-05); the Partner scope stays on.
