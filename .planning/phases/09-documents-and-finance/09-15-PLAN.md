Decisions carried in (locked): D-17 (one Admin page, Month/Year switch, tiles income, expense, balance in CZK, category table rolling children into the parent, by-client table with a "No client" row, bar chart of months in the selected year as a Filament chart widget; periods by the transaction date in Europe/Prague; sums in CZK from stored CZK amounts), D-12 (Admin-only).

Decisions made in this plan (recorded):
- The page uses Filament's `HasFiltersForm` (`period` month|year, `year`, `month`); the widgets use `InteractsWithPageFilters`, whose `pageFilters` property is client-controlled, so every widget and the page re-validate through `FinanceFilters::fromInput()` (pattern of `ReportFilters`, research Pattern 8).
- Widgets live under `app/Filament/Finance/Widgets`, outside the panel's widget discovery, so the pinned discovered-widget list of `PanelAccessTest` stays unchanged; each carries `#[AccessRule(Audience::AdminOnly, ...)]` and `EnforcesWidgetAccessRule`.
- In Year mode the chart shows that year; in Month mode the chart shows the year of the chosen month.
- The chart is the one place where minor units become numbers for Chart.js (whole CZK, research A16); tiles and tables format through `Money::format('cs')`.
- Sums use Eloquent `Transaction::query()->selectRaw(...)->groupBy(...)` (no `DB::table()`, research Pattern 8); the category rollup groups by `COALESCE(parent_id, id)` of the category.
- The "Bez klienta" label for `client_id IS NULL` (D-17 "No client" row).
- Navigation "Přehled financí", `navigationSort` 61.
