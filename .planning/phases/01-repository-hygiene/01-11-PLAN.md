A term written with diacritics therefore matches the same text without them, the reverse also holds, and composed and decomposed spellings match.
   - Matching is byte-wise after folding and does not depend on the system locale.
   - Letters of other scripts are compared exactly as written.
   - Accented letters in UTF-16 or legacy single-byte encodings such as Windows-1250 are not folded; only their ASCII letters match.
