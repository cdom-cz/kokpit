Decisions carried in (locked): D-05 (a confirmation after every payment credited to an invoice, partial or full, card or manual; "exactly one per payment" rests on this storage). Claude's discretion used here: the de-duplication (a webhook retry or a corrected payment must not send a second e-mail), enforced by the unique payment key and the cascade.

Planner assumption carried (defined in plan 13-06, recorded, easy to reverse): A-13-6, the five-minute grace window; it relies on the cascade of this table, so a payment deleted before the e-mail leaves takes its row with it.

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-13-02-1 (table, research Pattern 5): `payment_confirmations`: `id` uuid primary key default `uuidv7()`; `invoice_payment_id` uuid NOT NULL with the unique index `payment_confirmations_invoice_payment_unique` and the foreign key `payment_confirmations_invoice_payment_fk` to `invoice_payments (id)` ON DELETE CASCADE (added by `DB::statement`, like `card_payments_invoice_payment_fk`); `invoice_id` foreign uuid to `invoices` with the index `payment_confirmations_invoice_id_index`; `recipient` varchar(255) nullable; `language` char(2); `remaining_minor` bigint; `state` varchar(16) default `queued`; `attempts` integer default 0; `sent_at` timestamptz nullable; `failure_code` varchar(32) nullable; `timestampsTz()`. Named checks: `payment_confirmations_state_check` (queued, sending, sent, failed, skipped), `payment_confirmations_language_check` (cs, en), `payment_confirmations_remaining_check` (>= 0), `payment_confirmations_attempts_check` (>= 0), `payment_confirmations_sent_at_check` ((state = 'sent') = (sent_at IS NOT NULL)), `payment_confirmations_failure_code_check` (failure_code IS NULL OR state = 'failed'), `payment_confirmations_recipient_check` ((recipient IS NULL) = (state = 'skipped') AND (recipient IS NULL OR btrim(recipient) <> '')). No guard trigger: the job of plan 13-06 moves state and attempts freely (the `invoice_emails` precedent). `down()` drops the table. The confirmation never gets a column on `invoice_payments` or `invoices`: both refuse updates (research finding 3), and `invoice_emails` is not reused because its state drives the lock and the in-flight edit rule.
- R-13-02-2 (model and enum): `App\Domain\Invoicing\Models\PaymentConfirmation extends KokpitModel implements PartnerIsolated`, `use DeniesPartners`, table `payment_confirmations`, nothing fillable (forceFill only), no `$touches`, no activity log (the row is the log), relations `invoice()` and `payment()` (BelongsTo `InvoicePayment` by `invoice_payment_id`), casts `state` to the enum, `attempts` and `remaining_minor` to integer, `sent_at` to `immutable_datetime`; docblock in the style of `InvoiceEmail`. `App\Domain\Invoicing\Enums\PaymentConfirmationState`: Queued, Sending, Sent, Failed, Skipped (string-backed, values as the check; labels come in plan 13-08).
- R-13-02-3 (registries, in the same commit as the model, because the architecture and canary tests scan the models and fail in a commit that adds a model without its entries): morph alias `payment_confirmation` in `MorphMap::MAP`; `Gate::policy(PaymentConfirmation::class, AdminOnlyPolicy::class)` with a one-line comment; `PaymentConfirmation::class` in both lists of `ModelDeclarationTest` and in `partnerIsolatedModels()` of `CanaryRegistryTest`; in `CanaryRegistry::invoiceDocuments()` one sent confirmation row of the canary payment (written with `forceFill` like the canary payment itself) whose recipient is the canary joined to `example.com` at runtime, and the fixture entry `PaymentConfirmation::class` that calls `invoiceDocuments()`. The job registry entry follows with the job in plan 13-06.
- R-13-02-4 (tests that hold before and after the trigger): every case of `PaymentConfirmationSchemaTest` starts from a payment without a confirmation row. The helper records the payment through `RecordPayment::handle()` and then deletes, as the system, any confirmation row of that payment (a no-op before plan 13-06 wires the trigger, afterwards it removes the row the trigger wrote), and writes the rows a case needs as the system with `forceFill` or raw inserts. So the file stays green in every later plan.
