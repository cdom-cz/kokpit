Decisions carried in (locked): D-05 (the confirmation goes to the client, to the client's invoice e-mail, in the client's language, after every payment credited to an invoice, partial or full, card or manual; a partial payment gets the remaining amount, the final one the "paid in full" wording), D-06 (no attachment; number, amount paid, date and, for a partial payment, the remaining amount). Claude's discretion used here: the trigger point and the de-duplication (a webhook retry or a corrected payment must not send a second e-mail).

Planner assumptions (recorded, visible, easy to reverse; the owner may overrule each):
- A-13-1 (research open question 1): proforma payments get a confirmation too; credit notes never (RecordPayment refuses them); the carried copy of a proforma payment on the converted invoice never (`carry()` does not pass through `recordLocked()`). To reverse: skip `InvoiceKind::Proforma` in `QueuePaymentConfirmation::handle()`.
- A-13-5 (research open question 5): no "pay the remaining amount by card" button in a partial-payment confirmation; D-06 lists the content. To reverse: add the `PayLinkBlock` button to the view.
- A-13-6 (new, Claude's discretion): a grace window of 300 seconds (`QueuePaymentConfirmation::GRACE_SECONDS`) before the e-mail leaves. A payment deleted within it (`DeletePayment`) takes its row with it (the ON DELETE CASCADE of plan 13-02) and nothing is sent, so the usual correction (delete the mistaken payment, record the right one) mails one confirmation. A correction made after the e-mail left sends the corrected payment's own confirmation, because D-05 (locked) requires one after every credited payment. 300 s stays below the 600 s warning of the oldest-pending-job indicator. To reverse: set the constant to 0.

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-13-06-1 (queueing): `App\Domain\Invoicing\Actions\QueuePaymentConfirmation`, `final readonly`, constructor `InvoiceBalances`; `public const int GRACE_SECONDS = 300`; `handle(Invoice $locked, InvoicePayment $payment): PaymentConfirmation` throws `LogicException` outside a transaction, reads the client with `Client::query()->withTrashed()->whereKey($locked->client_id)->first()` (an archived client keeps getting confirmations), takes the language from the client's `invoice_language` (D-05 says the client's language; the research suggested the document language, the locked decision wins), takes the remaining amount from `InvoiceBalances::for([$id])` (null treated as 0), writes the `PaymentConfirmation` row of plan 13-02 with `forceFill` and, with a recipient, dispatches `SendPaymentConfirmation::dispatch($row->id)->afterCommit()->delay(now()->addSeconds(self::GRACE_SECONDS))`; without one it stores `skipped` and dispatches nothing. `public static recipientFor(Invoice $invoice): ?string` is the one recipient rule (also used by the record dialog in plan 13-08): the client's `invoice_email` trimmed, null when blank, longer than 255 characters, holding a control character or failing `email:rfc` (the rule of `QueueInvoiceEmail::cleanRecipient()`).
- R-13-06-2 (trigger, research Pattern 5): `RecordPayment` gains the constructor collaborator `private QueuePaymentConfirmation $confirmations`; `recordLocked()` calls `$this->confirmations->handle($locked, $payment)` after `$this->paymentLinks->reconcile($locked)` and before the return; the transaction guard and the actor rule at its top stay untouched; `carry()` gets nothing. The docblocks of the class and of `recordLocked()` say that every recorded payment queues one confirmation and a carried copy none. Not the Filament action, not the webhook handler: `recordLocked()` is the one write core of the manual path, the card match and the Admin's assignment.
- R-13-06-3 (job, the `SendInvoiceEmail` pattern without its lock): `App\Domain\Invoicing\Jobs\SendPaymentConfirmation`, `final`, extends `KokpitJob`, `#[Idempotent(how: '...')]` naming the row id, the conditional claim and the unique payment key; constructor `public readonly string $confirmationId`; `handle()`: a non-UUID id does nothing; the claim is one conditional update from queued to sending with `attempts + 1` (no invoice row lock: the job writes nothing but its own row); a row that is gone (its payment was deleted), sending, sent, failed or skipped is left alone; the stored recipient is checked again with the rule of `recipientFor()` and a refused one ends as failed with `invalid_recipient`; the mail is `PaymentConfirmationMail::forPayment()` sent unqueued with `Mail::to()->send()`; the sent mark is a conditional update from sending to sent with `sent_at`. On a throw the row goes back to queued and a transport failure is rethrown as a `RuntimeException` with the fixed message of `SendInvoiceEmail` (the provider answer can name the recipient, T-10-65); `failed()` marks a queued or sending row failed with `transport` or `unexpected` inside `runAsSystem()`. Constants `FAILURE_TRANSPORT`, `FAILURE_UNEXPECTED`, `FAILURE_INVALID_RECIPIENT`. No bell notice of its own: `KokpitJob` already reports a final failure through `ReportFailedJob`. The class never names the invoice lock-state enum, the invoice audit or the lock step of the document e-mail, and its docblock says the confirmation never locks.
