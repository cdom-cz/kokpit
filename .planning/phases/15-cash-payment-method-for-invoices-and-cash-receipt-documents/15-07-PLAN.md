Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-15-07-1 (forIssued): `$online` becomes `$noBank = ! $method->printsBankDetails()`; when true, `bank_snapshot` is set to null if one is stored (cash and online alike). The retake of the snapshot fires when the stored method did not print bank details (`! $storedMethod->printsBankDetails()`), so cash -> transfer refills like online -> transfer. The online availability refusal stays for online only.
- R-15-07-2 (issuedSwitch): a new first branch for a method that neither allows a card choice nor forces card payment (cash): an explicit `true` is refused under `card_payment_enabled` with `invoices.errors.card_payment_cash` (from 15-01), otherwise the switch is `false`. The online branch uses `forcesCardPayment()`. The transfer branch treats a stored cash method like a stored online method for the derived value (`! $storedMethod->allowsCardChoice()` -> client flag when card payment is available), and the WR-01 availability rule applies to it unchanged.
- R-15-07-3 (no Action change): `UpdateIssuedDocument` already deactivates the live link on `method_changed` and `IssuedEditFinisher::paymentLink` opens a new link only while `card_payment_enabled`; the tests prove it for cash. `PayPageState` is not changed (CONTEXT: the existing information page).
- R-15-07-4 (conversion): `ConvertProforma` passes no bank snapshot when the proforma's method does not print bank details, and the new invoice's Admin-only row gets `card_payment_enabled` from `$method->forcesCardPayment()` (false for cash and transfer, as today for transfer).
- R-15-07-5 (editor): in `EditInvoice` the helper `isOnline()` is replaced by `methodOf(mixed $state): DocumentPaymentMethod` (string or enum to enum, transfer for anything else); the account field and the issued bank state hide when the method does not print bank details (`invoices.edit_issued.bank_cash` "Doklad placený hotově neuvádí bankovní spojení." for cash, the online line unchanged); the card switch is disabled when the method does not allow a card choice, with helper `invoices.draft.helpers.card_payment_cash` "Doklad placený hotově nelze platit kartou."; `methodChosen()` sets the switch true for online, false for cash, and the client flag plus the preselected account when leaving online or cash for transfer.
