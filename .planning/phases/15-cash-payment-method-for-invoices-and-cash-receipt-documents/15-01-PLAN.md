<objective>
Make cash a real payment method of a document on the draft and issue path: the database accepts it and ties it to "no bank snapshot", the draft rules keep the card switch off and need no account, and the issuer stores no bank snapshot and no pay token. The phase requirements P15-01 to P15-12 are registered, and the assumptions of the phase, including the owner answers of 2026-10-27, are recorded where the owner sees them.

Purpose: P15-01 (cash as third method), P15-02 (storage half: no bank snapshot stored); D-01, D-02 (storage), D-03 (draft half), D-04 (default unchanged), D-05.
Output: one migration, the extended enum with three predicates, the draft and issue branches, REQUIREMENTS.md entries, tests. The printed and displayed side of D-02 (PDF cash box, e-mail text, Admin and Partner detail) is plan 15-04.
</objective>

Decisions carried in (locked): D-01 (third case `Cash` in `DocumentPaymentMethod`, the column and its CHECK extended, no new column; credit note none; costly), D-02 (cash hides every payment detail, `bank_snapshot` NULL like online), D-03 (cash excludes card payment), D-04 (chosen like the other methods; default stays transfer), D-05 (currency independent, no account needed).

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-15-01-1 (enum): `case Cash = 'cash'` after `Online`. Three predicates replace the method comparisons that decide bank details and the card switch: `printsBankDetails(): bool` (true only for Transfer), `allowsCardChoice(): bool` (true only for Transfer: the Admin decides the switch), `forcesCardPayment(): bool` (true only for Online). Cash answers false to all three, which means: no bank details, switch forced off. The class docblock gains the cash sentence (Phase 15, D-01, D-02, D-03). Czech label `enums.document_payment_method.cash` = "Hotově".
- R-15-01-2 (checks): new migration `2026_10_27_000100_add_cash_to_document_payment_method.php` drops and re-adds, under the same names, `invoices_payment_method_check` with `payment_method IS NULL OR payment_method IN ('transfer', 'online', 'cash')` and `invoices_issued_fields_check` with `... (kind = 'credit_note' OR (due_date IS NOT NULL AND (payment_method IN ('online', 'cash')) = (bank_snapshot IS NULL)))`, every other part verbatim from `2026_10_24_000200_add_payment_method_to_invoices.php`. No change to `kokpit_guard_invoice_row()` (the method is already an open column). `down()` throws a `RuntimeException` while any row has `payment_method = 'cash'`, else restores both checks verbatim from the Phase 14 migration. Header comment in the style of the Phase 14 migration names D-01, D-02 and why no guard change is needed.
- R-15-01-3 (draft rule): in `DocumentPayment::forDraft()` the card switch of a cash draft is `false`; an explicit `card_payment_enabled === true` with cash is refused under `card_payment_enabled` with the new message `invoices.errors.card_payment_cash` ("Doklad placený hotově nelze platit kartou."). The account id is still stored when sent (harmless, never printed); none is required.
- R-15-01-4 (issue): `IssueChecks` adds `bank_missing` only when the method prints bank details; `DocumentIssuer::bankSnapshot()` returns null unless the method prints bank details; `DocumentIssuer` writes the Admin-only row with the switch `effective(kind, true)` for online, `false` for cash, `effective(kind, stored)` for transfer (`CardPaymentSwitch::effective()` is an instance method of the injected `App\Domain\Payments\Support\CardPaymentSwitch`, resolved as `app(CardPaymentSwitch::class)` the way `DocumentIssuer` line ~277 does today, never called statically), and fills `bank_account_id` only for a method that prints bank details; the pay token follows the switch as today. Until 15-04 adds the cash payment box, `InvoicePdfData::payment()` returns null for a cash document (no snapshot), so its PDF prints no payment box at all and still no bank data.
