- R-15-14-1 (column): the table query eager-loads `cashReceipt` and `carriedFrom.cashReceipt`; a new column `cash_receipt` after `method`: the receipt number of the payment, or of the source proforma payment for a carried copy (with the description `cash_receipts.admin.carried` "ze zálohové faktury"); a badge for the state: `admin.state.open` "Otevřený" gray, `admin.state.locked` "Uzamčený" success, `admin.state.cancelled` "Zrušený" danger; a tooltip on a cancelled receipt with the reason from `CashReceiptAudit::cancelReason()`; a dash for a payment without a receipt. The existing confirmation column keeps showing skipped / queued / sent, so an open receipt with a skipped confirmation reads as not sent (AS-10).
- R-15-14-2 (actions): one `ActionGroup` per row with: `downloadCashReceipt` (redirect to `CashReceiptPdfUrl::download()` built at click time; visible when the row's own receipt has a PDF); `editCashReceipt` (modal: `received_on` date, `purpose` textarea max 500, customer and supplier name, street, city, postal code, country, company ID and VAT ID prefilled from the snapshots; visible when the own receipt is open and not cancelled; calls `UpdateCashReceipt`); `cancelCashReceipt` (danger, modal with a required reason textarea 10..500, the heading naming the number; visible when the own receipt is not cancelled; calls `CancelCashReceipt`); `unlockCashReceipt` (modal with a required reason; visible when the own receipt is locked and not cancelled; calls `UnlockCashReceipt`); `regenerateCashReceiptPdf` (visible when the own receipt has no PDF; calls `RegenerateCashReceiptPdf`). Each action checks `InvoiceResource::canAccess()`, re-reads the payment through `$this->invoice()->payments()` and its receipt, shows a `ValidationException` as a danger notification (as `deletePaymentAction()` does) and dispatches `invoice-payment-saved`. A carried copy offers only the download of the source receipt.
- R-15-14-3 (delete): `deletePaymentAction()` is also disabled while the payment's own receipt is not cancelled, with the tooltip `admin.delete_blocked` "Nejdříve zrušte příjmový doklad :number."; the docblock of the class gains the receipt paragraph (Phase 15, D-08, D-13). The Action-level refusal of 15-12 stays the authority.
