Implement the storno path of D-08: the Admin cancels a receipt with a recorded reason, which marks it cancelled for good and re-renders its PDF, and only then may the payment be deleted; DeletePayment refuses earlier, and no document delete path can remove the receipt's stored PDF as evidence.

Purpose: P15-10 (no deletion before the cancel; number never reused), P15-12 (cancelled receipt kept, marked, audited); D-08, D-11 (the stored PDF is evidence). Research Pitfall 2 (lock order, nothing deleted) and Pitfall 3 (the correction flow now burns a number on purpose). AS-1 proven end to end, AS-16 (reason in the Admin-only log).
Output: `CancelCashReceipt`, the refusal in `DeletePayment`, the receipt PDF protection in `DeleteDocument`, tests.
</objective>

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-15-12-1 (cancel): `CancelCashReceipt::handle(User $actor, CashReceipt $receipt, string $reason): CashReceipt`; `REASON_MIN = 10`, `REASON_MAX = 500` (as `UnlockInvoice`); `Gate::forUser($actor)->authorize('update', $receipt)` first (a Partner gets the base denial, the Admin passes `KokpitPolicy::before()`); the trimmed reason outside the bounds throws `ValidationException` under `reason` with `cash_receipts.errors.reason_length`. One `DB::transaction`: lock the invoice row, then the payment row when the link is not empty, then the receipt row; a cancelled receipt throws under `receipt` with `cash_receipts.errors.already_cancelled`; `forceFill(['cancelled_at' => now()])->save()` (a single-row model save, so the guard sees one statement that sets only `cancelled_at` and `updated_at`); `CashReceiptAudit::cancelled($locked, $actor, $clean)`; `StoreCashReceiptPdf::handle($actor, $locked->refresh())` re-renders with the cancelled band. No confirmation row is touched (AS-1: a queued confirmation is still sent, without attachment, by 15-10).
- R-15-12-2 (deletion guard): in `DeletePayment::handle()`, right after the carried-copy refusal and before the income is locked: read `CashReceipt::query()->where('invoice_payment_id', $locked->id)->lockForUpdate()->first()`; when it exists and is not cancelled, throw `ValidationException` under `payment` with `cash_receipts.errors.payment_has_receipt` and `['number' => $receipt->number]`. A cancelled receipt is left alone: the database FK clears its payment link when the payment row goes (15-02). Neither the receipt nor its PDF is deleted. Class docblock: the step list gains "2b. a payment with a receipt that is not cancelled is refused (Phase 15, D-08)" and the lock order becomes document, payment, receipt, income; the `@throws` line names the new key.
- R-15-12-3 (PDF protection): `DeleteDocument` refuses a file owned by a `cash_receipt` unless `confirmedIssuedPdf` is true, with `DomainException(__('cash_receipts.errors.pdf_protected'))`, in any lock state (the PDF of a receipt is evidence; only `StoreCashReceiptPdf` of 15-09 replaces it, and it passes the flag). The class docblock gains one sentence naming Phase 15, D-08.
- R-15-12-4 (race with the send): the cancel takes the invoice, payment and receipt row locks in that order; the confirmation job of 15-10 takes only the receipt row lock, so the two serialize on the receipt row without a deadlock (R-15-10-3). The class docblock of `CancelCashReceipt` says so.
