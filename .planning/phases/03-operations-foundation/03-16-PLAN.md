Make the System page tell whether background work is healthy: real failed-jobs, oldest-pending and scheduler indicators against fixed documented thresholds, a scheduler heartbeat and a worker heartbeat job on the schedule so a dead worker or scheduler becomes visible, and the failed-job alert linking to the page (D-12, D-13).

FND-10 is partially delivered by design in Phase 3: the last rate date, unprocessed webhooks and unsent invoice e-mails indicators remain placeholders that Phases 8, 10 and 11 replace through `HealthIndicatorRegistry::replace()`.
Output: three real indicators, heartbeats, the heartbeat job and schedule, thresholds, tests and the alert link assertion.
</objective>

Verified in Laravel 13.35 before planning: `RedisQueue::creationTimeOfOldestPendingJob($queue)` reads the head of the ready list (`createdAt` of the payload, creation time, not readiness time); `app('queue.failer')` is a `CountableFailedJobProvider` for the `database-uuids` driver; `tests/Arch/QueryEscapeHatchTest.php` forbids raw table queries in `app/`.

Thresholds (D-12 starting values kept, planner decision documented in `config/kokpit.php`): `scheduler_heartbeat_error_after` 180, `oldest_pending_warning_after` 600, `oldest_pending_error_after` 1800 (seconds), `failed_jobs_warning_at` 1. Delayed jobs: a job dispatched with a delay shows its creation age once it becomes ready (research Pitfall 10); the base job's backoff tops out at 300 s, below the warning; later phases that delay jobs longer must account for it (documented on the indicator and in the KokpitJob docblock from 03-13). The heartbeat lives in the cache with `Cache::forever`; a cache flush or Redis restart shows Error for up to one minute and heals itself (research Pitfall 18, accepted and documented).
