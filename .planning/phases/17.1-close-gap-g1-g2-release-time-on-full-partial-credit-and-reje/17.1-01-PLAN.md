<objective>
Close audit gap G1: once partial credit notes have taken back every creditable quantity of an invoice, the credit note that took back the last quantity returns all time entries of that invoice to unbilled and unlocked, in its own locked transaction, through the release path the storno already uses. The invoice is not cancelled.

Purpose: core value (no unbilled time slips through unnoticed). Today a sent invoice credited in full by partial credit notes keeps its time billed and frozen, and the only manual release path is closed once the invoice is sent. The database link guard is the reason: it lets billed time leave a locked invoice only after a storno credit note, so the guard gains a third acceptance reason mirrored from `CreditableQuantities::for()`.
Output: one migration (guard function only), the release in `IssueCreditNote::partial()`, docblocks, Feature tests, raw-SQL guard tests and a Concurrency test with its worker.
</objective>

Planner finding (why a migration, recorded): `kokpit_guard_time_entry_link()` as replaced by `database/migrations/2026_10_22_000200_follow_invoice_lock_in_time_entry_link.php` accepts an unbilling (billed -> unbilled, link dropped) only after an issued credit note with `cancels_invoice` names the document, or while `kokpit_invoice_accepts_time_changes()` says the document is open. A sent invoice is locked, so `EntryBilling::release()` after the last partial credit note would raise KP001. The phase therefore replaces the guard function (no table, column or data change). This does not contradict D-08: D-08 forbids a data migration and a repair, not a function replacement.

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-17.1-01-1 (helper): `kokpit_invoice_fully_credited(document uuid) RETURNS boolean LANGUAGE plpgsql`, volatile like `kokpit_invoice_accepts_time_changes`. It reads `kind` and `state` of the document row `FOR SHARE`; a missing row, a kind other than `invoice` or a state other than `issued` answers false. Otherwise it answers true exactly when the document has at least one line (`invoice_items.invoice_id = document`) with `quantity > 0` and no such line is left whose `quantity` plus the sum of the `quantity` of every credit line pointing at it is still above zero; a credit line points at it when its `credited_item_id` is the line id and its document has `kind = 'credit_note'`, `state = 'issued'` and `credited_invoice_id = document`. Lines with a negative quantity are never creditable and are ignored. This is the figure of `CreditableQuantities::for()` (sums of exact `numeric(14,4)` quantities), written in SQL for every writer.
- R-17.1-01-2 (guard): `kokpit_guard_time_entry_link()` is replaced in place with `CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION`; the trigger `time_entries_link_guard` is not re-created. Every branch is copied verbatim from the `up()` body of `2026_10_22_000200_follow_invoice_lock_in_time_entry_link.php` except the unbilling branch (`IF OLD.billing_state = 'billed' THEN`): its comment names the three reasons, its condition gains `AND NOT kokpit_invoice_fully_credited(OLD.invoice_id)` between the storno `NOT EXISTS (...)` and `AND NOT kokpit_invoice_accepts_time_changes(OLD.invoice_id)`, and the RAISE text becomes "time billed by a document is released only while the document is open for changes, after an issued storno credit note cancels it, or once issued credit notes took back every line" with `ERRCODE = 'KP001'`. `up()` creates the helper first, then the guard. `down()` restores the guard body of the `2026_10_22_000200` `up()` verbatim, then runs `DROP FUNCTION IF EXISTS kokpit_invoice_fully_credited(uuid)`. The header comment follows `2026_10_22_000200` (`declare(strict_types=1)`, anonymous `return new class extends Migration`): what changes against the 10.1 body and why (G1, D-01 to D-03), that `cancels_invoice`, `invoices_one_storno_per_invoice` and `Invoice::isCancelled()` are untouched (D-03), that no row is changed (D-08), reversibility costly, and the usual limits (table owner can disable triggers, `session_replication_role = replica` skips them).
- R-17.1-01-3 (release): in `IssueCreditNote::partial()`, right after `$credit = $this->issueCredit($actor, $locked, $quantities, false);` and before `$this->paymentLinks->reconcile($locked);`, the entries are released when `$this->creditedInFull($locked)` is true, through the existing private `releaseEntries($locked)`. New `private function creditedInFull(Invoice $locked): bool` reads `CreditableQuantities::for($locked)` and answers true when no quantity `isPositive()`; its docblock says it runs only after a partial credit note was issued in this transaction under the row lock taken by `lock()`, so the new credit note counts and at least one line had something to credit. Nothing is written on the invoice and `cancels_invoice` stays false (D-03). `storno()` is not changed.
- R-17.1-01-4 (docblocks): `IssueCreditNote` class docblock: the paragraph on lines 60-62 is rewritten to say that the storno releases the time, and that a partial credit note releases it only when, together with the earlier ones, it leaves nothing creditable on any line (Phase 17.1, D-01, D-02); the release comes after the issue because the link guard demands the issued credit note (`kokpit_invoice_fully_credited()` for the partial case); the invoice is not cancelled (D-03); payments and their income stay (D-16). `partial()` docblock: the time stays billed until the credit notes take back every line, and the one that takes back the last quantity returns all entries to unbilled. `releaseEntries()` docblock: "of the cancelled or fully credited invoice", and the guard lets them go because the credit note is issued by now. `EntryBilling`: the `release()` bullet of the class docblock and the `release()` method docblock name the third case (the last partial credit note that takes back everything, Phase 17.1 D-01).
- R-17.1-01-5 (concurrency): `tests/Concurrency/CreditNoteConcurrencyTest.php` with worker `tests/Concurrency/credit-worker.php`, modelled on `PaymentDeletionConcurrencyTest.php` and `payment-worker.php` (barrier, env, `_test` database refusal, JSON result line, typed outcomes, cleanup with disabled user triggers inside one transaction and an all-triggers-enabled check). Each round arranges its own sent invoice: four finished one-hour entries without a project of one fictional client with an hourly rate (as in `ReservationConcurrencyTest.php`), on a Prague day of their own per round so a round never picks up the released entries of an earlier one; a draft from that day through `CreateDraft` (`source` time, `without_project` true, `grouping` project), issued through `IssueDocument`, then locked with `InvoiceFixtures::lock()`.
