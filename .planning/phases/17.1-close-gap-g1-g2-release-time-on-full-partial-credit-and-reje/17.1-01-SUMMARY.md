# Phase 17.1 Plan 01: Release time on a fully partially-credited invoice Summary

**The partial credit note that takes back the last creditable quantity of a sent invoice returns all its time to unbilled and unlocked in the same locked transaction, backed by a new database guard reason, without cancelling the invoice.**

## Accomplishments

- Migration `2026_10_30_000200_release_time_of_fully_credited_invoice.php`: `kokpit_invoice_fully_credited(uuid)` (SQL mirror of `CreditableQuantities::for()`, reads the document `FOR SHARE`, ignores draft credit notes and negative lines) and `kokpit_guard_time_entry_link()` replaced in place with the unbilling branch accepting a third reason; the 10.1 body is otherwise verbatim; `down()` restores the 10.1 body and drops the helper.
- `IssueCreditNote::partial()` calls the new `creditedInFull()` after `issueCredit()` on the row locked by `lock()` and, when true, `releaseEntries()` (the path the storno uses) before the payment link reconcile. Nothing is written on the invoice. Docblocks of `IssueCreditNote` and `EntryBilling` state the Phase 17.1 rule.
- Tests: tracer on a locked invoice (row compared before and after, storno refused with `credit_nothing`), open-invoice exhaustion, all-at-once call, 0.0001 h left, payments untouched, EUR with CNB snapshot, PDF failure rollback; raw-SQL guard cases (partial line, draft credit note, no lines, proforma, credit note, frozen snapshot, `down()`/`up()`); Concurrency test with 3 scenarios x 5 rounds and the `credit-worker.php` child process.
