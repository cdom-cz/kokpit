<objective>
Close gap 1 of 14-VERIFICATION.md (CR-01, blocker): in the open issued editor the Admin can keep a document's stored bank account through a currency change by picking it again. Today `EditInvoice::issuedValues()` drops `bank_account_id` from the payload whenever it equals the stored account, so after a currency change (which preselects by the new currency) the re-picked stored account never reaches `UpdateIssuedDocument`; `DocumentPayment::forIssued()` then preselects for the new currency and either refuses every save with `bank_account_required` (no account in that currency) or silently prints the other currency's account.

The tracer: an issued CZK transfer invoice printing account "First CZK" is switched to USD (no USD account), the Admin picks "First CZK" again and saves; the save succeeds and both the snapshot and the stored id name "First CZK".

Purpose: the roadmap clause "the account can also be chosen separately and independently of the currency" holds for issued documents (P14-04, P14-06; D-05, D-06, D-09).
Output: the corrected `issuedValues()` and two regression cases in `IssuedEditorTest.php`.
</objective>
