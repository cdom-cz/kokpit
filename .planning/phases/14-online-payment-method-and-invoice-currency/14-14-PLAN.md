<objective>
Close gap 3 of 14-VERIFICATION.md (WR-02): a paid proforma always converts, and the invoice inherits the proforma's bank account. Today `ConvertProforma::inheritedAccount()` returns null when the proforma's account was removed from the settings; the draft then falls back to the first account of its currency, so with no such account `IssueChecks` raises `bank_missing` and the atomic conversion of a fully paid proforma fails, and with another account of the currency the invoice silently prints an account the customer never paid to.

The tracer: a transfer CZK proforma paid in full, its account removed and only a EUR account left, converts; the invoice prints the proforma's bank snapshot and records the proforma's account id.

Purpose: P14-04 (no transfer document is left without an account it can print, and none prints an account nobody chose) and P14-03 (the account stays tied by id); D-05, D-06, OA-2 (the invoice inherits the proforma's method and account), OA-7 (an issued transfer document carries a bank snapshot).
Output: an optional bank snapshot on `IssueContext`, the matching parameter of `IssueChecks`, the fallback in `DocumentIssuer::bankSnapshot()`, the inheritance in `ConvertProforma`, and three conversion cases.
</objective>

Decisions carried in (locked): D-05 (the account is stored separately and never silently re-picked), D-06 (an account in another currency is allowed; the snapshot is taken from the chosen account at issue), OA-2 (the converted invoice inherits the proforma's payment method and bank account; `ConvertProforma` reconciles the link after the carries), OA-7 (an issued transfer document carries a bank snapshot, an online one none).

Rulings carried in and amended here: R-14-09-1 ("a converted proforma inherits payment_method and, when still configured, bank_account_id") is amended by R-14-14-4 below: the id is inherited as stored, and an id that is no longer configured makes the invoice print the proforma's snapshot instead of another account. The ruling of plan 14-04 ("a stored but removed account id is no account, never re-picked") is what makes the unfiltered id safe.

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-14-14-1 (`IssueContext`): a fourth, optional constructor parameter `public ?array $bankSnapshot = null` (phpdoc `array<string, mixed>|null`, the shape of `Invoice::$bank_snapshot`), "the bank snapshot to copy" for a document that must print the bank details of another document, in the style of `$exchangeSnapshot`; `IssueContext::now()` is unchanged. The class docblock names the converted invoice as its one user.
- R-14-14-2 (`IssueChecks`): `blockers()` and `assertIssuable()` gain a trailing `?array $bankSnapshot = null` parameter (phpdoc as above); `bank_missing` is added only when the draft is a transfer document, `DocumentPayment::account($draft)` is null AND `$bankSnapshot` is null. Every existing caller passes fewer arguments and is unchanged (EditInvoice line 312, the tests).
- R-14-14-3 (`DocumentIssuer`): `issue()` passes `$context->bankSnapshot` to `assertIssuable()` and to `bankSnapshot()`; `bankSnapshot(Invoice $draft, ?array $inherited = null)` returns null for a credit note or an online draft as today, the settings copy of `DocumentPayment::account($draft)` when it resolves, else `$inherited` when it is given, else the existing `bank_missing` refusal. `writeAdminRow()` keeps its code; its docblock says that a converted invoice printing the proforma's snapshot keeps the proforma's account id, or none.
- R-14-14-4 (`ConvertProforma`): `inheritedAccount()` returns the proforma's stored `invoice_admin.bank_account_id` as it is (null when empty), without asking the settings, so `DocumentPayment::account()` of the draft treats a removed id as no account and never re-picks another one; `handle()` issues the draft with `new IssueContext($actor, IssueContext::today(), null, $snapshot)` where `$snapshot` is the locked proforma's `bank_snapshot` for a transfer proforma and null for an online one. The exchange snapshot stays null: the conversion is priced with the CNB list of its own issue date (ruling R-22-2). The snapshot comes only from the row locked at the start of the transaction.
