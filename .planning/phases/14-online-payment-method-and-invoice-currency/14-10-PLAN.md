- R-14-10-1 (fields in issued mode): the three fields of plan 14-08 lose their `! $this->issuedMode()` condition (still invoice and proforma only); in issued mode the online option is disabled while card payment is unavailable unless the document is already online; the toggle follows the same disabled rule as in a draft. The issued fill (`mutateFormDataBeforeFill()`) sets `payment_method` to the stored method, `card_payment_enabled` to the stored switch (true for online), and `bank_account_id` to the stored Admin-only id when it is still configured, else null.
- R-14-10-2 (`issuedValues()`): passes `payment_method` and `card_payment_enabled` when they are keys of `$data` (dehydrated), and `bank_account_id` only when it differs from the value the page filled (recomputed at save with the same rule as R-14-10-1), so a document whose stored account was removed, or a legacy one without an id, is not refused on an unrelated save (the Action keeps its printed snapshot, research Pattern 2); `reload_bank` as today. - R-14-10-3 (bank section of the issued editor): the `issued_bank_account` entry shows the printed lines (`InvoiceColumns::bankLines()`) while nothing changes; "Po uložení se použije účet:" with the chosen account's summary when the chosen account differs from the stored one, the method moves to transfer, the currency changed or the refill is marked; and the line "Online úhrada: doklad bankovní údaje netiskne." for an online method in the form. `refillBankAction()` marks `reload_bank` when the form's chosen account, or the preselection of the document currency, resolves to a configured account, else notifies `invoices.errors.bank_account_required`. The `bank` error branch of `updateIssued()` is removed (no Action raises it any more); `payment_method`, `bank_account_id` and `card_payment_enabled` errors show under their fields.
