<objective>
Give the document its payment method in the database and open, on purpose, exactly the columns the phase needs on an open issued document: the method, the card switch, a once-only pay token and the chosen account id. This is the database tracer of the phase: a raw-SQL switch of an open issued invoice to online is accepted, the same write on a locked invoice is refused, and a raw insert defaults to transfer.

Purpose: P14-01 (method per document, default transfer, none on a credit note), P14-06 (database half: open while open, frozen when locked); D-01, D-02, D-03 (database half), D-05 (account stored apart from the currency), D-09; research Pattern 1 items 1-6, Pattern 6, Pitfalls 1 and 4, Runtime State Inventory (backfill); owner amendments OA-1 (card switch editable while open, reversing the frozen-at-issue rule) and OA-7 (the online carve-out of Phase 10 D-12: an issued online document has a NULL `bank_snapshot`, an issued transfer document must have one), both recorded in the amendments block of 14-CONTEXT.md.
Output: two migrations, the document payment method enum and its labels, the model casts, and the schema, isolation, architecture and card-switch tests moved to the new rules in the same commits.
</objective>
