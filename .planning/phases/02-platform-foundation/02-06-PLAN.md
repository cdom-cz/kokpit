<objective>
Deliver the single sequence allocator (D-11, D-12): a counters table locked with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` inside the caller's transaction, so numbers are gap-free and duplicate-free, proven by real parallel PHP processes on PostgreSQL and by a mutation run that shows the proof can fail.

Purpose: FND-05. Task keys (Phase 5) and invoice numbers (Phase 10) depend on it, and issued numbers can never be changed afterwards (D-11 is rated one-way), so the row contract is confirmed by the owner before the migration ships.
Output: `number_sequences` migration, `SequenceAllocator`, feature tests, the concurrency harness and the `Concurrency` Pest suite.
</objective>
