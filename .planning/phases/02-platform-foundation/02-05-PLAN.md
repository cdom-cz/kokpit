<objective>
Build the one `Money` value object every persisted amount and every call site will use (D-08), with exact arithmetic delegated to `brick/money` and exactly one HALF_UP rounding method applied once per invoice line amount (D-09), decimal exchange and per-minute rates kept outside `Money` until the rounded result (D-10), and an Eloquent cast over two columns.

Purpose: FND-04. Money mistakes are silent: a float, a second rounding or a currency mix produces plausible wrong totals. Each rule here has a failing test next to it.
Output: `Money`, `MoneyCast`, unit, feature and arch tests, and the `Arch` Pest suite.
</objective>
