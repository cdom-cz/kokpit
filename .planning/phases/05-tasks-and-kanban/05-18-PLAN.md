<objective>
Close two seams where a form and a domain Action disagree about what may be written: CR-01 (the Admin edit page refuses every save of a task whose assignee or requester was deactivated since, although UpdateTask keeps an unchanged person) and G-1 (CreateTask drops a Partner's status, priority and people but not tags).

Purpose: TA-01 says the Admin edits tasks with an assignee; today one offboarded Partner account blocks every later edit of that Partner's tasks. TA-07 and D-04 say a Partner supplies only the content of a task; tags are Admin-only (research A1, UI-SPEC U-4), and the drop must hold before Phase 7 lets an external caller reach CreateTask without the Partner create page in front of it.
Output: the people options fix and its Livewire tests, the Partner tags drop and its Action test, each with a recorded mutation run.
</objective>
