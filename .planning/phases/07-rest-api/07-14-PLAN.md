Facts measured while planning (PHP 8.5 in DDEV, Carbon as installed):
- `0001-01-01` at Prague midnight is `0000-12-31 23:02:16+00:00` (historic local mean time, UTC year 0), so that day cannot be a filter bound; `0001-01-02` begins in UTC year 1.
- Re-parsing the text `10000-01-01` with `createFromFormat('!Y-m-d', ...)` throws `InvalidFormatException`; building the same midnight from the year, month and day components gives `9999-12-31 23:00:00+00:00` (CET), which PostgreSQL stores. So `9999-12-31` is a supported day once `midnight()` stops re-parsing a formatted string.
- `9999-12-31T23:59:59-12:00` is UTC year 10000; `0001-01-01T00:00:00+02:00` is UTC year 0.

Decisions made in this plan (recorded, Claude's discretion):
- The envelope lives in `TimerClock` ("the single clock of every time write") as `isStorable(CarbonInterface $value): bool`: true when the instant's UTC year is between 1 and 9999. Both `TimeEntryInput` and `PragueDay` ask it, so the range is stated once.
- Tests assert year bounds and storability, not the exact historic local-mean-time seconds, so a tzdata update cannot break them.
- An out-of-range day in the panel's custom period filter is dropped like any other invalid day (the existing `PragueDay::day()` null path), with no new message.
