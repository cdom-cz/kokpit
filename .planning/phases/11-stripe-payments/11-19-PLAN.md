Decisions carried in (locked): D-13 (assign to a document writes invoice_payments and income through the RecordPayment rules; mark as resolved with a note; record a money return as a manually entered expense linked to the duplicate payment, no Stripe API call and no refund automation), D-14 (a matched payment is corrected by DeletePayment on the document), Phase 10 D-16.

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R11-19-1 (D-13): `AssignCardPayment::handle(User $actor, CardPayment $record, array $data): CardPayment` authorises `update` on the record; data keys `invoice_id`, `paid_on` (a Prague day not after today, default the received day), `income_date` (default `paid_on`; ignored on a proforma), `category_id` (default the system income category; ignored on a proforma), `note` (at most 500). In one transaction: lock the document first, then the record; refuse a resolved record with `card_payments.errors.already_resolved` (key `card_payment`), a matched or refund record with `card_payments.errors.not_assignable`, a document in another currency with `card_payments.errors.currency_mismatch` under `invoice_id` ("Měna platby (:currency) nesouhlasí s měnou dokladu."); then `RecordPayment::recordLocked($locked, new PaymentFacts(record amount, paid_on, PaymentMethod::Card, income_date, category_id, note), $actor)` (its own refusals, for example a cancelled document, stand), then the record gets resolution assigned, `resolved_at`, `resolved_by`, `assigned_invoice_id`, `invoice_payment_id`.
- R11-19-2 (D-13): `ResolveCardPayment::handle(User $actor, CardPayment $record, string $note): CardPayment` authorises `update`, locks the record, refuses resolved and matched records, requires a trimmed note of 1 to 500 characters (field error under `note`), sets resolution noted with the note.
- R11-19-3 (D-13, U-12, Phase 10 D-16): `RecordPaymentReturn::handle(User $actor, CardPayment $record, array $data): CardPayment` with keys `amount` (a positive decimal not above the payment amount, field error `card_payments.errors.return_above_payment` "Částka vrácení nesmí být vyšší než částka platby."), `returned_on`, `category_id` (an expense category), `note`; in one transaction: lock the record, refuse resolved records and anything but duplicate or unmatched, book the expense through `RecordTransaction::handle($actor, ...)` (type expense, the payment currency, description "Vrácení platby kartou", the client of the record's document when there is one), then resolution returned with `return_transaction_id` and the note. No Stripe call.
- R11-19-4 (UI-SPEC Surface H): `ViewCardPayment::getHeaderActions()` returns `assignCardPayment` ("Přiřadit k dokladu"; accent on unmatched, gray on duplicate), `resolveCardPayment` ("Označit jako vyřešené"; accent on duplicate and refund, gray on unmatched) and `recordPaymentReturn` ("Zaznamenat vrácení peněz"; gray; duplicate and unmatched only), each visible only on an unresolved record of a type it applies to, with the modal fields, live warnings, info callout, submit and cancel labels of the UI-SPEC copy; the assign select lists issued, not cancelled invoices and proformas in the payment's currency as "číslo, klient, zbývá :remaining", preselecting the record's document; each action re-checks `CardPaymentResource::canAccess()`, calls its Action, then shows the success toast, dispatches `card-payment-resolved` and refreshes the record; a refusal with `already_resolved` shows the danger toast "Platba už byla vyřešena" and refreshes.
