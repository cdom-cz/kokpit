Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R11-16-1 (U-10, Open Question 2): `CardPaymentAlerts::duplicate(CardPayment)`, `unmatched(CardPayment)`, `refund(CardPayment)` each call `AdminAlerter::alert('card-payment:'.$record->id, new OperationalAlert($title, $body, CardPaymentResource::getUrl('view', ['record' => $record]), __('payment_notices.open_payment'), __('payment_notices.mail_subject', ['title' => $title])))` with the copy of `lang/cs/payment_notices.php`; amounts formatted with `Money::format('cs')`; the unmatched `:reason` is the reason's enum label. The key per record means no two records are ever merged into one notice.
- R11-16-2 (research Anti-patterns: alerts must not depend on the queue): `RecordCardPayment` registers `DB::afterCommit()` callbacks for a duplicate or unmatched record; `RecordRefundEvent` for a refund record. `AdminAlerter` is synchronous and never throws.
- R11-16-3 (Pitfall 11, D-14): `StripeEvent` reads for `refund.created` the refund object's `id`, `amount` (positive int), `currency` and `payment_intent`. `RecordRefundEvent::handle(StripeEvent $event): ?CardPayment` (LogicException outside a transaction) finds the non-refund card payment with that payment intent, locks its document when there is one, and calls `CardPayment::recordOnce()` with type refund, the refund amount and currency, the event, refund and payment intent ids, the document and link of the related payment (none when the refund came first), `received_at` now; a null answer is a repeat. Nothing else is written: no payment, no transaction, no document change.
- R11-16-4: `ProcessStripeWebhook::handlers()` maps `refund.created` to `RecordRefundEvent::class`.
