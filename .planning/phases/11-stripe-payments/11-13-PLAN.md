Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R11-13-1 (research "Reading a verified event", Pitfall 6): `StripeEvent::fromPayload(array $payload): self` reads only `id`, `type`, `created`, and of `data.object`: `id` (session), `payment_status`, `payment_intent` (a string), `payment_link`, `amount_total` (a positive int), `currency` (upper-cased, `^[A-Z]{3}$`), `metadata.invoice_id` and `metadata.payment_link_row` (kept only when they are UUIDs). Anything malformed becomes null; nothing is typed through SDK objects.
- R11-13-2 (Pattern 5, Open Questions 1 and 6 adopted): `RecordCardPayment::handle(StripeEvent $event): ?CardPayment`, LogicException outside a transaction. A session not `paid`, or without a positive amount and a valid currency, records nothing (an unpaid session waits for its async event). The link is found by `metadata.payment_link_row`, else by the Stripe link id; the document by `metadata.invoice_id`, else by the link (D-10). The document is locked. Classification in order: no link or no document, or a link of another document than the metadata names: unmatched `undetermined` with no document; a document that is not an issued invoice or proforma: `undetermined`; cancelled: unmatched `cancelled`; remaining 0: duplicate `already_paid`; currency other than the link's: unmatched `currency`; amount other than the link's offered amount or other than the remaining amount: unmatched `amount`; otherwise matched. The record is claimed first with `CardPayment::recordOnce()` (type, reason, amount, currency, the link's offered amount and currency, the event, session, payment intent and link ids, the document when determined, the link row, `received_at` now); null means a repeat and ends the handler with nothing written (D-12, D-14).
- R11-13-3 (D-10, plan 11-02): a matched record then calls `RecordPayment::recordLocked($locked, new PaymentFacts(amount, paidOn, PaymentMethod::Card, paidOn, null, null), null)` with `paidOn` the Prague date of the event's `created` (today when absent), sets `invoice_payment_id` on the record, and relies on the reconcile inside `recordLocked()` to deactivate the link (reason paid). A missing CNB list for a foreign-currency income throws, the transaction rolls back and the job's retry and failure path of plan 11-04 applies (failed_at, System page).
- R11-13-4 (UI-SPEC Surface I): `CardPaymentMatchedNotification` is a non-queueable database notification in the Filament format (success), title "Přišla platba kartou", body "Doklad :number byl zaplacen (:amount)." or for a proforma "Záloha :number byla zaplacena (:amount).", action "Otevřít doklad" to the document detail; sent with `notifyNow` to every Admin from `DB::afterCommit()`, so a rolled-back match notifies nobody. No Stripe id, payer name or e-mail.
- R11-13-5 (plan 11-04 R11-04-6): `ProcessStripeWebhook::handlers()` maps `checkout.session.completed` and `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded` to `RecordCardPayment::class`; the job calls the handler inside its transaction after locking the webhook row.
