<objective>
Keep every Stripe link honest: whenever what a document owes changes, its live link is deactivated in the same transaction and then at Stripe; the Admin can regenerate a link for the remaining amount (the old one deactivated first) or deactivate one by hand.

Purpose: PA-04 in full; D-07, D-08; research Pattern 3 (one reconcile invariant called from every transaction that changes what is owed, plus a sweep); UI-SPEC Surface E (the two link actions the detail page of plan 11-12 calls).
Output: `PaymentLinks::reconcile()`, its calls in `RecordPayment`, `IssueCreditNote` and `DeletePayment`, `DeactivatePaymentLinks` with its schedule, `RegeneratePaymentLink`, `DeactivatePaymentLink`, their refusal copy, the feature test.
</objective>

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R11-10-1 (Pattern 3): `PaymentLinks::reconcile(Invoice $locked): void` throws `LogicException` outside a transaction; locks the live link of the document (`PaymentLink::live()` with `lockForUpdate`), none: done; reads remaining through `InvoiceBalances::for()` inside the transaction (it sees the caller's uncommitted writes) and the cancellation with a fresh query, not a cached attribute; deactivates with `cancelled`, `paid` (remaining 0) or `amount_changed` (remaining different from the offered amount), setting `deactivated_at`; when the row has a Stripe id, dispatches `DeactivatePaymentLinks` for it after the commit. A pending row without a Stripe id is left to its own job, which sees the deactivation in its second lock (plan 11-07).
- R11-10-2 (call sites, D-08): `RecordPayment::recordLocked()` calls `reconcile()` after the payment and the income, so the manual path, the webhook match (plan 11-13) and the assignment (plan 11-19) are all covered; `IssueCreditNote::partial()` and `storno()` call it on the locked credited invoice after the credit note is issued; `DeletePayment::handle()` calls it after the deletions and after the flag is cleared. `carry()` does not: the invoice made by a conversion is issued with its switch off (plan 11-03) and has no link.
- R11-10-3 (Pattern 3, D-08): `DeactivatePaymentLinks extends KokpitJob`, `#[Idempotent(how: 'a row is done once remote_deactivated_at is set; setting active=false at Stripe twice is harmless')]`, constructor `public readonly ?string $linkId = null` (null: the sweep); `handle(StripeGateway $gateway)` takes up to 50 rows in state deactivated with a Stripe id and no `remote_deactivated_at` (only the given id when set), calls `deactivateLink()` outside any transaction and sets `remote_deactivated_at` with a conditional update; a `StripeGatewayFailure` is logged with the failure class and the row id only and the row is left for the next sweep. Scheduled `Schedule::job(new DeactivatePaymentLinks)->everyFiveMinutes()->name('kokpit-payment-link-sweep')->onOneServer()`.
- R11-10-4 (D-07, D-08, UI-SPEC Surface E): `RegeneratePaymentLink::handle(User $actor, Invoice $invoice): PaymentLink` authorises `update` on the invoice, then in one transaction locks the document; refuses with `payment_links.errors.not_regenerable` (key `payment_link`) unless the document is an issued invoice or proforma, not cancelled, with its frozen switch on and remaining above 0, and with `payment_links.errors.preparing` while the live link is pending; an active link is deactivated with `replaced` (remote deactivation after the commit); then `PaymentLink::open($locked, remaining, $actor)` and `CreatePaymentLink::dispatch()->afterCommit()`. `DeactivatePaymentLink::handle(User $actor, Invoice $invoice): void` authorises `update`, locks the document, deactivates the active link with `manual` (remote after the commit), and refuses with `payment_links.errors.no_active_link` otherwise.
