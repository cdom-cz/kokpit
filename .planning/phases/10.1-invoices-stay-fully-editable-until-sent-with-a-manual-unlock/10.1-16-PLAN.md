Deferred finding that needs the owner (record it, do not implement it): WR-04. The document e-mail is sent before the lock transaction, and a failure of the lock or audit step puts the row back to queued, so a retry mails the customer again. Every fix chooses a degraded state for the rare case "transport accepted, lock step failed", and each option touches a phase decision (D-01: a delivered document is locked; the 10.1-01 ruling that the sent mark and the lock share one transaction; the documented at-least-once guarantee T-10-64):
- A. keep the at-least-once resend (today's behaviour);
- B. never resend after the transport accepted: the row stays sending, the exception is reported and the Admins are told the document was sent but not locked; the document stays open until the Admin acts;
- C. persist the delivery first (a delivered marker or a new e-mail state; needs a migration of `invoice_emails_sent_at_check` and of the state CHECK and labels), then retry only the lock and audit step without resending; edits keep waiting while a delivered row is not locked; a permanent failure still needs an Admin decision.
The planner's recommendation for the owner is C as a bounded follow-up plan (about 2 tasks); the choice is the owner's.

Owner decision to record (2026-10-23, fingerprint accepted by the owner, reason T-10.1-18): a change of the bank account is audited by a non-reversible keyed fingerprint (`bank_fingerprint`, built by plan 10.1-14), the fingerprint before and the fingerprint after, with who and when, and not by the account values; the account number never enters the history, on purpose. It narrows the "values before and after" of IN-15 and D-13 for this one field. The checker of this round found the old plan text ("IN-15 not narrowed") in conflict with what plan 10.1-14 builds; Task 2 makes the requirement and the decision log say what the code does. It does not change the status of any requirement.

Out of scope of this round and deferred with a reason: WR-03 (cancelled invoices and converted proformas stay `open` in the database; consistent with success criterion 1, "storno never locks", the application refusals `cancelled_not_editable` / `converted_not_editable` and `kokpit_invoice_accepts_time_changes()` cover the application paths; database protection needs an owner decision between locking at storno/conversion and extending the guard predicates) and the informational IN-01 to IN-06.
