## Tests

### 1. Cold Start Smoke Test
expected: Stop any running app and queue worker, start from scratch (DDEV restart, migrations applied). The app boots without errors, the phase migrations complete, and the invoice list loads with live data.
result: pass

### 2. Edit an issued invoice before it is sent
expected: Open an issued but never sent invoice detail. An edit action opens the draft editor. Change a line and the printed note, save. Amounts are recomputed, the PDF is created again, a notice says nothing was sent, and the invoice number and kind are unchanged.
result: pass

### 3. Edit header fields of an open document
expected: In the editor of an open issued invoice or proforma, change the issue date, due date, language, customer/supplier lines and bank account. The issue date is refused when it falls outside the numbering period or out of order against its series neighbours; valid changes save.
result: pass

### 4. Send locks the document
expected: Send the e-mail of the open issued invoice. After the send succeeds the document is locked - the edit action is gone or refused, and the lock appears in the Admin-only history.
result: pass

### 5. Unlock a sent document with a reason
expected: On a sent invoice the Admin can unlock with a reason of 10 to 500 characters (shorter or longer is refused). The document is editable again, stays open until the next successful send locks it again, and both the unlock and the lock appear once in the history with the reason.
result: pass

### 6. Add and release tracked time on an open document
expected: On an open issued invoice add unbilled time (it becomes billed and priced at the issue date) and release a linked time entry (it returns to unbilled). A fixed amount brings its fixed-price hours. CZK total, PDF and audit follow.
result: pass

### 7. Change currency of an open document
expected: Change the currency of an open issued invoice. Time lines are re-priced at the issue date, CNB figures, bank account and PDF follow. A document with a payment or a credit note keeps its currency and the change is refused.
result: pass

### 8. Card payment link and QR follow an edit
expected: After editing the amount of an open invoice, the old Stripe payment link is deactivated and a new one is created for the amount owed; the regenerated PDF and QR code show the new amount. A paid session on an old-currency link is recorded as unmatched (currency), not booked.
result: [pending]

### 9. Edit history and warnings
expected: After the first send, every edit leaves one Admin-only "edit" row in the history tab (in Czech) with values before and after; a bank account change shows only a fingerprint, never the account itself. Lowering the total below what was paid or credited warns instead of blocking.
result: [pending]

### 10. Stale queued e-mail can be resent
expected: A document e-mail stuck as queued past the System page threshold no longer blocks editing; the Odeslani tab offers "Odeslat znovu" on that row.
result: [pending]

### 11. Partner sees nothing of the editing
expected: Logged in as a Partner, the invoice detail shows no edit or unlock action, no history, and no rates, prices or other clients' data.
