<objective>
Close gap 1 of 10.1-VERIFICATION.md (review CR-01, critical, money): a payer who completes the old Stripe Checkout session of a document whose currency was changed with the same figure must never have that payment booked, marked paid and recorded as income in the new currency.

Purpose: plan 10.1-03 relaxed `payment_links_invoice_currency_fk` so an open document can change currency (D-06, D-11); the webhook (`RecordCardPayment`) still compared the paid currency with the link only. This plan makes the document's current currency part of the match (the application rule the verifier asked for, with the webhook test) and restores, at the point where money is booked, a database pin between a card payment record and the payment it books (project constraint: integrity is enforced in the database, CONTRIBUTING: application code is not the only guard).
Output: the corrected currency step and docblock of `RecordCardPayment`; two webhook tests in `PaymentClassificationTest.php`; migration `2026_10_23_000100_pin_card_payment_currency_to_its_payment.php` applied to the DDEV development database; raw-SQL tests in `CardPaymentRecordTest.php`.
</objective>

Decisions carried in (locked, not revisited): D-06 with the owner ruling of 2026-10-23 (currency is editable on an open document; a document with a payment or a credit note keeps its currency, money received is not re-denominated), D-11 (on an amount or currency change the card link is deactivated and a new one created; a card payment already recorded keeps its record). Ruling of plan 10.1-03 (STATE.md): a payment link records the currency it offered and never changes; new links must match the document currency; `invoice_payments` keeps its composite key to `invoices (id, currency)`.

Why both layers: the old link row keeps its currency forever and Stripe does not cancel a Checkout session the payer already opened, so an old session can always arrive after a currency change. The application check classifies it correctly (unmatched `currency`, alerted, resolvable by the Admin through the existing overview). The database pin makes any other writer that tries to book a card payment in a currency other than the one paid fail loudly with KP001 instead of booking silently.
