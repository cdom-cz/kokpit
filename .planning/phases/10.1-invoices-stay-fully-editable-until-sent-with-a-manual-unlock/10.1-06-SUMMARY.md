# Phase 10.1 Plan 06: The issued editor Summary

**The existing draft editor now opens an open issued invoice or proforma from its detail, saves lines and notes through UpdateIssuedDocument, freezes credited lines, previews converted lines with the frozen quotes, and says that the PDF was created again, nothing was sent, and whether the total fell below what was paid or credited.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 75 min
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 10 (1 new)

## Accomplishments

- `EditInvoice` serves two modes with one form (R-10.1-06-1). Issued mode: title "Úprava dokladu :number", the kind badge and "Vystaveno, lze upravit", no header actions, "Uložit změny" and a gray "Zpět na doklad" link, the number shown read-only with its hint, the card switch and the bank row hidden, currency, language and the dates disabled, the add-time and add-fixed-amount buttons hidden, no relation tabs. `mount()` redirects any issued document that `IssuedDocumentRules::editBlocker()` refuses to the detail with the reason as a warning notification.
- Saving in issued mode sends `items`, `note` and `internal_note` only. A `document` error becomes a danger notification and `Halt`; `items.<n>.*` errors land under their rows; `total_not_positive` lands on the items table. After a save the page sends the success message, then one persistent warning per `IssuedDocumentRules::warnings()` entry, and redirects to the detail.
- Credited lines (R-10.1-06-2): quantity, unit and unit price are disabled with the hint "Dobropisovaná položka: měnit lze jen text.", and the row's delete button is hidden. The rule is read from `IssuedDocumentRules::creditedLineIds()` on every request.
- `DraftEditorPreview` (R-10.1-06-3): for an issued document a converted line is priced through `TimeLineConversion::fromSnapshot()`, and the summary's CZK figure comes from the stored rate, unit and list date of `invoice_admin`, with the detail's wording instead of the draft's "se zmrazí při vystavení".
- `ViewInvoice` (R-10.1-06-4): the `edit` action first, visible while `Invoice::isEditable()`, and two warning callouts (`total_below_paid`, `total_below_credited`) that follow `IssuedDocumentRules::warnings()`.
- `IssueFlowTest` and the other draft tests pass unchanged, so the draft path behaves as before.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The editor mode derived from the record on each request broke "issued in the meantime"**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (full `tests/Feature/Invoicing` run)
- **Issue:** the first version asked `$this->invoice()->isIssued()` on every request. A draft editor whose draft was issued in another tab lost its `issue` header action, and `IssueFlowTest` "refuses a document that was issued in the meantime without a second number" failed with `issueAction does not exist`.
- **Fix:** the mode is set once in `mount()` into a `#[Locked]` public property (Filament re-resolves the record on every hydrate, so a flag set in `resolveRecord()` was overwritten).
- **Files modified:** `app/Filament/Resources/InvoiceResource/Pages/EditInvoice.php`
- **Commit:** 2e061b2

**2. [Rule 1 - Plan vs. behavior] The disabled credited fields are dehydrated**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** the plan says a disabled field is not dehydrated and mutate fills it from the stored line, but also that a crafted request is "refused by the Action and shown as a field error". With a plain disabled field a crafted value would be dropped silently and the save would succeed.
- **Fix:** `->dehydrated()` on the three disabled inputs; mutate still fills them from the stored line when absent. The crafted change is now a field error on the row.
- **Files modified:** `EditInvoice.php`
- **Commit:** 2e061b2 (tests in fe7815c)

### Notes

- The plan's behavior list for Task 3 asks for `total_below_credited` after an edit below the credited amount. As documented by plan 10.1-12, A4 keeps a credited line whole, so an edit cannot reach it; the detail test sets the stored total directly, the way the Action test does.
- The plan's R-10.1-06-1 keeps "currency, language and dates read-only" and says nothing about the bank row or the relation tabs; hiding both is a small addition (see key-decisions), not a change of scope.
- The one existing test "redirects the edit address of an issued document to its page" was rewritten with `lockedIssued()` as the plan prescribes, and a twin shows an open document opening.

**Total deviations:** 2 auto-fixed (2 Rule 1). **Impact:** none on scope.
