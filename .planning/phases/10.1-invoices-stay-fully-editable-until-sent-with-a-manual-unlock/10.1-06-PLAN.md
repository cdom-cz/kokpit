Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-10.1-06-1 (issued mode of `EditInvoice`): `mount()` opens the editor for a draft as before and for an issued document only when `isEditable()`; any other issued document redirects to the view page and sends a warning notification with `invoices.errors.<IssuedDocumentRules::editBlocker()>`. In issued mode: title `invoices.edit_issued.title` "Úprava dokladu :number", heading with the kind badge and a badge `invoices.edit_issued.badge` "Vystaveno, lze upravit"; no header actions (no issue, no delete); form actions `invoices.edit_issued.save` "Uložit změny" and a gray link `invoices.edit_issued.cancel` "Zpět na doklad" to the view page; the document section shows the number and kind read-only with the hint `invoices.edit_issued.number_hint` "Číslo a druh dokladu se nemění.", hides the card switch and keeps currency, language and the dates read-only (plans 10.1-07 and 10.1-08 open them); the buttons that add time or a fixed amount stay hidden (plan 10.1-09 opens them); `mutateFormDataBeforeSave()` sends `items`, `note` and `internal_note` only; `handleRecordUpdate()` calls `UpdateIssuedDocument`, maps `items.<n>.*` errors to the repeater rows exactly as for a draft, and turns a `document` error into a danger notification and `Halt`; after the save the page redirects to the view page and sends `invoices.edit_issued.saved` "Změny jsou uložené. PDF bylo vytvořeno znovu, doklad nebyl odeslán." plus one warning notification per entry of `IssuedDocumentRules::warnings()` with `invoices.warnings.<key>`.
- R-10.1-06-2 (credited lines, A4): at mount the editor reads the ids of its lines that a credit line names; on such a row quantity, unit and unit price are disabled with the hint `invoices.edit_issued.credited_hint` "Dobropisovaná položka: měnit lze jen text." and the delete action of the row is hidden. A disabled field is not dehydrated, so `mutateFormDataBeforeSave()` fills it from the stored line, and the Action still refuses any change sent by a crafted request.
- R-10.1-06-3 (preview, Pitfall 6): `DraftEditorPreview` takes the document's state; for an issued document a generated line in another currency is priced through `TimeLineConversion::fromSnapshot()` with its stored `conversion_snapshot` and the summary's CZK figure uses the stored CNB trio of `invoice_admin`, never today's lists; the draft path is unchanged.
- R-10.1-06-4 (detail): `ViewInvoice` gains header action `Action::make('edit')` labelled `invoices.detail.actions.edit` "Upravit", url of the edit page, visible only when `isEditable()`, placed first; `banners()` gains warning callouts with `invoices.warnings.total_below_paid` and `invoices.warnings.total_below_credited` (amount formatted in `cs`) while `IssuedDocumentRules::warnings()` reports them.
