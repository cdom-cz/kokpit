# Phase 10.1 Plan 05: Card link, QR and readable history for an edit Summary

**An edit of an open issued document now renews the Stripe payment link for the amount owed (deactivated as amount_changed inside the transaction, new link created by the queued job after the commit), the regenerated PDF and QR code carry the new amount, and the history tab labels edit rows in Czech.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 25 min
- **Tasks:** 3 (Tasks 1 and 2 share one commit because the link step and its full test file were written together)
- **Files:** 5 (1 created, 4 modified)

## Accomplishments

- `IssuedEditFinisher::finish()` gained step 2 between the CZK total and the PDF: `PaymentLinks::reconcile()`, then under the link rule `PaymentLink::open()` and `CreatePaymentLink::dispatch()->afterCommit()`. The order is documented in the class docblock together with the rule that Stripe is called only by the queued job.
- A failed render is proved to leave lines, totals, link rows, the Admin-only row, the media rows and the stored objects untouched, with no Stripe call.
- `ActivityPresenter::attributeLabel()` resolves `items.<n>.<field>`, `customer.<field>` and `supplier.<field>` for the alias `invoice`; `lang/cs/kokpit.php` holds the new labels.
