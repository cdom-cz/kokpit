<objective>
The currency of an open issued invoice or proforma can be changed with everything that hangs on it: CNB figures of the issue date, re-converted time lines, the bank account and the card link, and is refused where money already moved in the old currency.

Purpose: IN-13 (currency), IN-14 (link and PDF follow the currency); D-06, D-11; research Pitfalls 2 and 6; assumption A7.
Output: the `currency` key of `UpdateIssuedDocument`, `TimeLineConversion::freezeAt()` extracted from `DocumentIssuer`, the currency staleness of `PaymentLinks`, the currency change in the issued editor, the tests.
</objective>

Assumptions adopted as the planned default (owner to confirm before execution):
- A7: a currency change is refused once a payment exists (money received in a currency is never re-denominated; the database key `invoice_payments_invoice_currency_fk` stays the backstop) and once a credit note names the document (credit notes copy the currency and the CNB trio of their invoice).
- A6 (as in plan 10.1-07): only a currency change re-takes the CNB figures; the issue date used is the stored one.

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-10.1-08-1 (`TimeLineConversion::freezeAt(Invoice $locked, string $day, bool $again = false): bool`): the body of `DocumentIssuer::convertTimeLines()` moved here without behaviour change for `$again = false` (convert time lines whose rate currency differs from the document and whose source has no snapshot; one rounding via `DraftPreview::convertLine()`; snapshot written; totals recomputed; `cnb_missing`, `items` and `total_not_positive` errors as before); with `$again = true` it also converts lines that already carry a snapshot, and restores a line whose rate is in the document currency to the exact rate price (`price_converted` false, snapshot null). `DocumentIssuer::issue()` calls `freezeAt($lockedDraft, $issueDate)`; a typed price is never converted in either mode.
- R-10.1-08-2 (`currency` key of `UpdateIssuedDocument`): when the value differs from the stored currency, in this order, before any write: the code must be a currency of the editor's option list; refuse with `currency_has_payments` "Měnu nelze změnit, k dokladu jsou zaznamenané platby." when any `invoice_payments` row exists, with `currency_has_credit_notes` "Měnu nelze změnit, k faktuře existuje dobropis." when any credit note (draft or issued) names the document, with `bank_missing` when `InvoiceSnapshot::bank($new)` is null, with `cnb_missing` (date of the issue date) when a needed list is missing; then the items (if sent) are written in the new currency through `ItemListWriter` with `$currencyChanged = true`, `TimeLineConversion::freezeAt($locked, $issueDate, true)`, the row gets `currency` and `bank_snapshot`, and the `invoice_admin` trio and CZK total are taken again through `ExchangeRateConverter::rateFor($new, $issueDate)` (all four null for CZK). All errors under `currency`. The finisher then reconciles the link.
- R-10.1-08-3 (`PaymentLinks`): the stale reason of a live link is `AmountChanged` also when the link's currency differs from the document's (the amount comparison alone could match by accident).
- R-10.1-08-4 (editor): in issued mode the currency select is editable with the existing `currencyChosen()` / `changeCurrency` dialog; `DraftEditorPreview` prices generated lines for a currency other than the stored one with the lists of the stored issue date (not today), so the dialog shows what the save stores; the Action's refusals show under the currency field.
