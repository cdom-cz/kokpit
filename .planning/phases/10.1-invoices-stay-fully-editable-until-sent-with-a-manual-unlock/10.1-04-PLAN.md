<objective>
The domain write path of an open issued document: items and printed note are edited under the invoice row lock, amounts are computed with the one rounding rule, the PDF is replaced, nothing is sent, and the rules that keep credit notes, cancelled and converted documents, credited lines and frozen conversions consistent are enforced. The in-flight e-mail rule, the queue lock, the warnings below paid or credited and the post-send edit audit are plan 10.1-12 (split off by the plan checker for the context budget).

Purpose: IN-13 (items), IN-14 (PDF replaced, nothing sent); D-04 (no hard block), D-05, D-09, D-10; research Patterns 4 and 5, Pitfalls 5, 6.
Output: `UpdateIssuedDocument`, `ReplaceIssuedPdf`, `IssuedDocumentRules` (`editBlocker`, `assertEditable`), `IssuedEditFinisher`, `ItemListWriter` (extracted from `UpdateDraft`), `TimeLineConversion::fromSnapshot()`, the error copy and the tests.
</objective>

Assumptions adopted as the planned default (owner to confirm before execution):
- A1: credit notes are never edited.
- A3: a cancelled invoice and a converted proforma are not editable.
- A4: a credited line keeps quantity, unit and price and cannot be deleted; its text stays editable.
- A8 (in-flight e-mail) and A12 (edit rows carry item texts and party data) moved to plan 10.1-12 with the code they govern.
