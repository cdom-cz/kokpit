<objective>
The domain write path of an open issued document: items and printed note are edited under the invoice row lock, amounts are computed with the one rounding rule, the PDF is replaced, nothing is sent, the rules that keep credit notes, cancelled documents and in-flight e-mails consistent are enforced, and every edit after the first send is audited.

Purpose: IN-13 (items), IN-14 (PDF replaced, nothing sent), IN-15 (edit audit); D-04, D-05, D-09, D-10, D-13; research Patterns 4 and 5, Pitfalls 3, 5, 6, 9.
Output: `UpdateIssuedDocument`, `ReplaceIssuedPdf`, `IssuedDocumentRules`, `IssuedEditFinisher`, `ItemListWriter` (extracted from `UpdateDraft`), `TimeLineConversion::fromSnapshot()`, the queue lock, `InvoiceAudit::snapshot()/edited()`, the error copy and the tests.
</objective>

Decisions carried in (locked): D-04 (edits with payments or credit notes allowed, warning when totals fall below paid or credited, no hard block), D-05 (items editable, one HALF_UP rounding), D-09 (PDF regenerated and replaced, no confirmation prompt, no version history), D-10 (nothing sent automatically), D-13 (post-send changes audited with before and after; never-sent documents behave like drafts).

Assumptions adopted as the planned default (owner to confirm before execution):
- A1: credit notes are never edited.
- A3: a cancelled invoice and a converted proforma are not editable.
- A4: a credited line keeps quantity, unit and price and cannot be deleted; its text stays editable.
- A8: an edit is refused while an e-mail of the document is `queued` or `sending` and younger than `kokpit.health.unsent_invoice_email_stale_after` seconds (by `updated_at`); an older row counts as failed, so it never blocks forever; if such a stale row is still sent later, it attaches the PDF linked at that moment (the edited one) and locks the document.
- A12: the explicit edit rows carry item texts and party data in the Admin-only log (values cut to 80 characters by `ActivityPresenter`), an owner-approved exception to the T-10-30 "free text is never logged" precedent; the bank snapshot, the pay token and the internal note are never in the payload.
