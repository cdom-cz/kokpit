Decisions carried in (locked): D-06 (dates, customer and supplier snapshots, bank account and language editable; the currency follows in plan 10.1-08), D-08 (number never changes; one-way, settled), D-13 (post-send changes audited).

Assumptions adopted as the planned default (owner to confirm before execution):
- A5: a new issue date must not be after today (Prague), must give the same allocator scope key as the old date under the current pattern of its series (`NumberingSettings::patternFor()->scopeKey()` at Prague noon of each date), and must not be before the issue date of the previous issued document of the same kind nor after the issue date of the next one (neighbours ordered by `issued_at`).
- A6: an issue-date change keeps the CNB trio, the CZK total and the frozen conversions; the editor warns about it for a document not in CZK or with a converted line.
- A9: customer and supplier data are free fields of this document's snapshots plus a refill from the current client card or supplier settings; `vat_payer` of the supplier snapshot is kept as stored; the appearance snapshot and the VAT mode are not editable.
- A14: in non-payer mode the taxable supply date stays null and hidden.

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-10.1-07-1 (header keys of `UpdateIssuedDocument`, each optional): `issue_date` and `due_date` (Prague `Y-m-d` through `PragueDay`, never null for an issued document; a credit note never gets here), `taxable_supply_date` (only null accepted in non-payer mode, error `taxable_supply_not_payer`), `language` (`InvoiceLanguage`), `customer` and `supplier` (arrays checked by `SnapshotInput`), `reload_bank` (strict boolean: the bank snapshot is taken again through `InvoiceSnapshot::bank($locked->currency)`, `bank_missing` when null). The written row uses `forceFill`; number, kind, VAT mode and appearance are never written. Errors keyed `issue_date`, `due_date`, `language`, `customer.<field>`, `supplier.<field>`, `bank`.
- R-10.1-07-2 (`IssuedDocumentRules::assertIssueDate(Invoice $locked, string $newDate, string $today): void`): A5 with errors under `issue_date`: `issue_date_future` (existing copy), `issue_date_scope` "Datum vystavení musí zůstat ve stejném období číselné řady jako původní datum.", `issue_date_order` "Datum vystavení musí ležet mezi :previous a :next, daty sousedních dokladů řady." (dates written `j. n. Y` through `IssueChecks::czechDate()`, a missing neighbour leaves that bound open); the due date check reuses `due_before_issue`.
- R-10.1-07-3 (`SnapshotInput`): `final class App\Domain\Invoicing\Support\SnapshotInput` with static `customer(array $input, array $stored): array` (fields `name` required, `street`, `city`, `postal_code`, `country` two capital letters, `company_id`, `vat_id`; trimmed, empty to null; the length limits of `ClientInput::attributes()` for the corresponding client columns) and `supplier(array $input, array $stored): array` (fields `name` required, `street`, `city`, `postal_code`, `country`, `company_id`, `vat_id`, `email`, `phone`, `website`, `registration_note` with the limits of `SupplierSettings::rules()`, `company_name` mapped to `name`; `vat_payer` copied from `$stored`). Unknown keys are ignored; a field absent from the input keeps the stored value. `ValidationException` keyed `customer.<field>` or `supplier.<field>`.
- R-10.1-07-4 (editor): in issued mode the issue date, due date and language become editable (concrete values, no derived defaults), a section `invoices.edit_issued.parties` "Odběratel a dodavatel" shows the customer and supplier fields with actions `invoices.edit_issued.refill_customer` "Načíst z karty klienta" and `invoices.edit_issued.refill_supplier` "Načíst z nastavení dodavatele" that fill the form from `InvoiceSnapshot::customer($client)` and `supplier()` (nothing is written until "Uložit změny"), the bank snapshot read-only with the action `invoices.edit_issued.refill_bank` "Načíst bankovní účet z nastavení" that sets `reload_bank`; after a save that changed the issue date of a document not in CZK or with a converted line a warning notification `invoices.warnings.issue_date_rates_kept` "Kurz ČNB a převedené položky zůstávají z původního data vystavení."; the taxable supply date stays hidden in non-payer mode.
