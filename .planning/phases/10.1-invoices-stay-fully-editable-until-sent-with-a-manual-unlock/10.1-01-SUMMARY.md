# Phase 10.1 Plan 01: Send-driven lock of an issued document Summary

**An issued invoice or proforma now stays open and correctable until a successfully sent e-mail locks it, and PostgreSQL enforces both the open and the locked state against every writer, raw SQL included.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 50 min
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 14 (plus the deferred-items note)

## Accomplishments

- Migration `2026_10_22_000100_add_lock_state_to_invoices.php`: `invoices.lock_state`, the three CHECKs, the A2 backfill (credit notes and documents with a sent e-mail locked, the rest open, `updated_at` untouched), `kokpit_guard_invoice_row()` / `invoices_row_guard`, the three child guards re-keyed on the lock, and `kokpit_guard_invoice_admin_issued()`. `down()` restores the frozen-at-issue model and is round-trip tested.
- The row guard refuses any change to the number, variable symbol, kind, client, state, VAT mode, appearance snapshot, document links, storno marker and issue time of an issued document (open or locked), refuses every DELETE of an issued row, refuses all writes to a locked row, and opens a locked row only under `kokpit.invoice_unlock` naming that document (never for a credit note). It also refuses a currency change of a document that any credit note names (D-06 owner ruling).
- `SendInvoiceEmail` marks the row sent and locks the invoice in one transaction that locks the invoice row first; failed, queued and already-locked paths leave the invoice as it was.
- `DocumentIssuer` writes `Open` for an invoice or proforma and `Locked` for a credit note; `InvoiceLockState`, `Invoice::isLocked()`, Czech labels, `InvoiceFixtures::lock()` / `lockedIssued()`.
- Tests: new raw-SQL matrix (`InvoiceLockGuardTest`, 24 tests incl. pinned open list, backfill and down/up), `InvoiceLockTest`, lock cases in `SendInvoiceEmailTest`; the Phase 10 frozen-state tests were migrated (assertions rewritten on a locked document with open twins), none deleted.
