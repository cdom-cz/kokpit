# Phase 10.1 Plan 02: Unlock a sent document with a reason, audit every lock Summary

**The Admin can reopen a sent invoice or proforma from its detail with a recorded reason of 10 to 500 characters, the document stays open until the next successful e-mail send locks it again, and every lock and unlock lands once in the Admin-only history.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 55 min
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 10

## Accomplishments

- `UnlockInvoice` (R-10.1-02-1): authorises through the policy (Admin only), trims and measures the reason, locks the invoice row first, refuses a draft or an unlocked document, an issued credit note, a cancelled invoice and a proforma that has its invoice, then sets the transaction-local `kokpit.invoice_unlock` setting to that id, opens the row through a model save, clears the setting and writes the audit row, all in one transaction.
- `InvoiceAudit::locked()` and `::unlocked()` (R-10.1-02-2): one row each through the activity log, with `lock_state` before and after and, for the unlock, the trimmed reason. Nothing else is in the payload.
- `SendInvoiceEmail` writes `InvoiceAudit::locked($invoice, $row->sender)` inside the lock transaction, only when it actually moved the document from open to locked (R-10.1-02-3). Queued, failed, given-up and resent rows write none.
- `ViewInvoice`: a third badge with the lock label on an issued invoice or proforma (gray when locked, info when open), the `open_for_edits` info callout, and the `locked` warning callout carrying the `unlock` action with a required reason textarea and the 10 and 500 character rules. A refused reason shows under the field; a document that changed in the meantime shows a danger notification (R-10.1-02-4).
- Czech copy for the errors, the dialog, the banners and the history labels, including `invoice_edited` for plan 10.1-05 (R-10.1-02-5).
- `tests/Arch/InvoiceUnlockPathTest.php`: the unlock literal appears under `app` only in `UnlockInvoice` and under `database/migrations` only in the lock migration; the `InvoiceLockState::Locked` token appears under `app` only in `SendInvoiceEmail`, `DocumentIssuer` and `Invoice`; the scanner proves itself on a temporary directory.

## Decisions Made

- Followed rulings R-10.1-02-1 to R-10.1-02-5. The plan text says "not issued or a credit note -> credit_note_locked, a draft -> not_locked"; implemented as: a draft (including a draft credit note) gets `not_locked`, an issued credit note gets `credit_note_locked`.
- `UnlockInvoice` exposes `REASON_MIN` and `REASON_MAX` so the dialog rules and the Action cannot drift.
