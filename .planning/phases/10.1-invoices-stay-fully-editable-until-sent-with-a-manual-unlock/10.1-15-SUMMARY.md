# Phase 10.1 Plan 15: Stale queued e-mail rows Summary

**A queued document e-mail whose job was lost now ages out at the System page threshold: the edit goes through again, the job never sends such a row late (it gives it up as `stale` under the invoice row lock), and the Odeslani tab offers Odeslat znovu on it (review WR-01).**

## Accomplishments

- `InvoiceEmail::staleBefore()` is the one definition of the stale cutoff (`kokpit.health.unsent_invoice_email_stale_after`, at least 1 second).
- `IssuedDocumentRules::emailInFlight()` counts a queued or sending row only while its `updated_at` is after the cutoff; the `email_in_flight` docblock bullet is updated. A younger queued row still refuses the edit (D-01 unchanged).
- `SendInvoiceEmail::claim()` runs in one transaction: it locks the invoice row first, claims a fresh queued row (queued to sending, one attempt), and otherwise moves a still-queued row at or before the cutoff to failed with the new class `FAILURE_STALE = 'stale'` and sends the one bell notice (the notice part of `giveUp()` was extracted into `tellAdmins()`). Docblocks list the new class and the lock order.
- `InvoiceEmailsRelationManager::effectiveState()` reads queued and sending rows past the cutoff as failed, so the resend action is offered on them.
- No migration; `invoice_emails.failure_code` is a free string.

## Known limitations (accepted visibility, plan context)

A queued row whose job was lost reads as failed, with "Odeslat znovu", only on the document itself (the Odeslani tab). A lost job never runs, so the stale give-up and its bell notice do not happen for it. The System page indicator (`UnsentInvoiceEmailsIndicator`) keeps showing it as waiting and the e-mail filter of the invoice list keeps it under queued, both from the stored state, until the Admin resends it from the document. Plan 10.1-16 carries this note into the WR-01 row of `10.1-REVIEW-DISPOSITION.md`.

A queued row that waits longer than the threshold behind a long queue backlog or a worker outage is also given up as stale when the worker finally reaches it; the Admin resends it by hand (reversible, by design).

Review finding WR-04 (mail sent before the lock transaction) is untouched: `markSentAndLock()` and the catch are unchanged; deferred by plan 10.1-16.
