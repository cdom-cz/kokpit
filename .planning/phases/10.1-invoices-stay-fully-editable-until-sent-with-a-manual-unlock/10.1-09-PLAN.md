Assumptions adopted as the planned default (owner to confirm before execution):
- A3: cancelled invoices and converted proformas take no time changes.
- Releasing time from an issued document keeps its lines (as Phase 10 D-05 does for drafts); the Admin edits or deletes the lines in the editor, which recomputes the totals.

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-10.1-09-1 (one Action per intent, two lifecycles): `AddEntriesToDraft::handle()`, `ReleaseEntries::handle()` and `AddFixedAmount::handle()` keep their names and signatures. After locking the document: a draft follows the existing path unchanged; an issued document runs `IssuedDocumentRules::assertEditable()` and the issued path; anything else keeps the `not_draft` refusal. The class docblocks say so. The issued path takes `$before = InvoiceAudit::snapshot($locked)` first and ends with `IssuedEditFinisher::finish($actor, $locked, $before)`; totals are saved with model logging disabled when `InvoiceAudit::everSent()` (the R-10.1-04-3 rule).
- R-10.1-09-2 (add time, issued path): claim through `EntryReservation::claim()` (reservation is allowed by the link guard while the document is open), build the lines with `TimeItemGrouping::lines()` for the document's stored issue date instead of today, append with `DraftLines::appendTime()`, convert the new foreign-rate lines with `TimeLineConversion::freezeAt($locked, $issueDate)` (lines without a snapshot only), bill the claimed rows at once with `EntryBilling::bill($claimed, TimerClock::now())` in id order (reserve and bill are two statements, as the guard requires), refresh the totals. A missing CNB list of the issue date is `cnb_missing` under `currency` with that date.
- R-10.1-09-3 (release, issued path): the requested entries billed by this document are locked in id order and freed through `EntryBilling::release()`; the lines are not touched.
- R-10.1-09-4 (fixed amount, issued path): as R-10.1-09-2 with `DraftLines::appendFixedAmount()` and, with `link_time`, the fixed-price entries claimed and billed at once.
- R-10.1-09-5 (UI): `InvoiceEntriesRelationManager::isReadOnly()` is true unless the document is a draft or `isEditable()`; the title stays "Navázaný čas" for an issued document; after an add or release on an issued document the success notification also says "PDF bylo vytvořeno znovu, doklad nebyl odeslán." (`invoices.edit_issued.saved` reused) and the owner page is refreshed through its existing event; the issued editor shows "Přidat nový čas" and "Přidat pevnou částku" again. `lang/cs/kokpit.php` `time.errors.invoice_locked` becomes "Záznam patří k vystavené faktuře :number. Uvolnit ho lze na faktuře, dokud není odeslaná, nebo dobropisem."
