Decisions carried in (locked): D-05 (rates overview is an Admin page with a manual "download today's rates" action and a "backfill history" action with a from-to range; backfill runs queued, is idempotent, and days without a list resolve to the previous valid rate). Phase 3 D-10 (`KokpitJob`, `#[Idempotent]`, timeout chain job < supervisor 300 s < `retry_after` 330 s, asserted by `HorizonConfigTest`).

Decisions made in this plan (recorded):
- The manual download calls `DownloadCnbRates` synchronously (one request of at most 15 s) and is never gated by the working-day calendar; a failure shows a danger notification with the Czech reason and raises no Admin alert (the Admin is looking at it).
- Backfill: `BackfillCnbRates(string $from, string $to)`, `#[Idempotent]`, `#[Timeout(240)]` (below the 300 s supervisor), one `year()` request per year from the year of `from - 10 days` to the year of `to`, storing every list with `validFor <= to` (whole years are cheap and D-02 stores everything; the 10-day margin covers the list before a holiday block at the start of the range).
- Limits in `config/kokpit.php`: `rates.backfill.earliest` = `2000-01-01`, `rates.backfill.max_years` = 10.
- Overview navigation "Kurzy ČNB" in the Správa group, sort 94 (next to the System page).

Fictional data only: year payloads in tests come from `CnbFixtures` with invented rates. Run `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
