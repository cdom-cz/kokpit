Decisions carried in (locked): D-03 (publication after 14:30 Prague; scheduled after publication, hourly retries until about 18:00; values in `config/kokpit.php`), D-04 (final failure of the day triggers the Phase 3 alert: synchronous e-mail plus bell, throttled, queue-independent; the last known rate stays in use). UI-SPEC Surface G: alert title, body with dates only, action "Otevřít kurzy ČNB" to Surface A, one alert per day.

Decisions made in this plan (recorded, research open question 2 recommendation adopted):
- The daily download is a synchronous scheduled command, not a queued job, so a dead worker cannot swallow the alert (roadmap success criterion 2).
- Own `CzechPublicHolidays` (11 fixed days, Good Friday from 2016, Easter Monday; Easter by the anonymous Gregorian algorithm), no `ext-calendar` (research Alternatives). Assumption A5: CNB publishes on every Czech working day and on no public holiday (verified for 2023-2025 by the researcher).
- Only `--final` alerts, when the attempt threw `CnbDownloadFailed` or a `QueryException`, or when the day is a working day and no list dated today is stored after the attempt. Throttle key `cnb-rates:<Y-m-d>`.
- The alert action URL is the rates overview route (`filament.admin.resources.exchange-rates.index`, built by plan 08-13) resolved with `Route::has(...)`, like `ReportFailedJob` resolves the System page; until that route exists the alert carries no action. `OperationalAlert` gains an optional action label so the button reads "Otevřít kurzy ČNB" instead of the System page label; existing alerts keep their label.
- The body uses `kokpit.rates.alert.body` with `:date` and `:last`, or `alert.body_none` when no list was ever stored.
- A manual run from the rates overview (plan 08-13) calls the Action directly and is never gated or alerted.

Fictional data only: CNB answers in tests come from `CnbFixtures` with invented rates. Run `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
