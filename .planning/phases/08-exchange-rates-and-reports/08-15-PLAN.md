Decisions carried in (locked): D-02 (store the whole daily list with the unit amount), D-01/D-05 (weekend and holiday days resolve to the previous list; that resolution is a lookup in plan 08-04, never a stored copy). Phase 2/3 conventions: UUID v7 default `uuidv7()`, `timestamptz`, named constraints, `numeric(20,10)` rates with a `decimal:10` cast, `DeniesPartners` plus `AdminOnlyPolicy`, `DB::table` banned in `app/`.

Decisions made in this plan (recorded):
- Why this is its own plan: `tests/Arch/ModelDeclarationTest.php` (exact model list, a policy for every PartnerIsolated model) and `tests/Isolation/CanaryRegistryTest.php` (a fixture for every PartnerIsolated model) fail the full suite the moment a model exists without its registrations, and the post-wave gate runs the full suite. The model and its registrations therefore land together here, in wave 1; the CNB download of plan 08-01 builds on them in wave 2.
- A stored list is never rewritten: CNB lists are immutable once published, so a conflict does nothing (research Pattern 2). The model has no fillable attributes; rows are written only by the insert-or-ignore of `DownloadCnbRates` (plan 08-01) and the backfill job (plan 08-08), and by explicit test fixtures.
- `ExchangeRate` is `PartnerIsolated` with `DeniesPartners`: the CNB data is public, but it is finance-like and closed to Partners like every rate.

Fictional data only: every rate, currency code and date in tests is invented; no real CNB list is copied. Run `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
