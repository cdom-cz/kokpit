Uses from plans 08-04 and 08-07: `Money::hourlyRateFor()`, `CnbConverter::toCzk()`, `ExchangeRateMissing`, `ReportFilters` (`to`, `startUtc()`), `TimeReportReader::entries()`, `ReportEntry`, `EntryAmounts::of()`, `TimeReport::build()` with `summary` and `tree`, `TimeReportPage` and its view.

Decisions carried in (locked): D-07 (fixed-price projects: hours, the agreed fixed price and the effective hourly rate; no per-entry amount; reversibility costly), D-08 (effective hourly rate = fixed price / all worked hours of the whole project, not only the period, shown in client currency and CZK, with the price and the hours visible next to it).

Decisions made in this plan (recorded):
- Assumption A3: "all worked hours of the whole project" = every entry of the project, billable or not, finished or running, at any date (the same figure as `TimeTotals::forProject()['worked']`); a test pins the equality.
- Assumption 1 (adopted research recommendation for open question 1): the CZK value of the price uses the CNB rate of `min(period end, today)` in Prague, so a past report does not change from day to day; the CZK hourly rate is derived from the converted price (`Money::hourlyRateFor(priceCzk, seconds)`); the page shows the conversion day.
- The block lists every project whose `project_billing.billing_type` is `fixed_price` and that has entries in the report or is the filtered project. Whole-project seconds come from one grouped query over all entries of those projects and the prices from one query, so the block costs a constant number of queries.
- Assumption 3 stays: a task-level fixed price inside an hourly project shows hours only (no per-task effective rate).
- Plan 08-10 (next wave) consumes `TimeReportReader::entries()` and `EntryAmounts::of()`; their signatures stay unchanged here.

Fictional data only. Run `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
