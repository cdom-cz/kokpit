Root cause (08-VERIFICATION.md gap 2, 08-UI-REVIEW.md Pillar 2): `time-report.blade.php` sets `white-space: nowrap` on every numeric cell. The Název header has no width or minimum, and the table floor was 60rem with eight columns, so the name column gets the leftover width and titles break per word. Levels differ only by indent (24px each) and weight. The owner's options were a wider or sticky name column, single-line truncation with a tooltip, collapsible levels collapsed by default, fewer or grouped columns, and a clearer hierarchy. This plan and 08-27 adopt all of them except single-line truncation: a two-line clamp keeps more of a title readable for the same row height.
