- XLSX numbers are real numeric cells: seconds as integers, hours as seconds / 3600 to four places, amounts from the exact `Money::toMajor()` string converted to a number only at the cell (a spreadsheet stores doubles anyway); the CSV keeps exact strings. Text cells stay `SpreadsheetCells::text()`.
