  <context>Dompdf is the decided PDF engine (Phase 3 spike, CONTEXT.md). AGPL-3.0 compatibility question, LGPL-2.1-only versus LGPL-2.1-or-later: with -or-later, the library may also be taken under LGPL-3.0, which is drafted to combine with GPL-3.0 and, through section 13 of both GPL-3.0 and AGPL-3.0, with an AGPL-3.0 work. With -only, that route is closed; what remains is that LGPL-2.1 lets a work that only uses the library carry its own licence as long as the library's own terms are honoured (library source and its modifications under LGPL, the user can replace the library; section 6), and section 3 lets a copy of the library be converted to the ordinary GPL version 2 or, once a newer GPL version has appeared, to that newer version, which reaches GPL-3.0 and hence the AGPL-3.0 combination. Kokpit uses Dompdf unmodified as a Composer dependency, distributed as source, so the library stays separately replaceable. The planner's screen: both readings look compatible, the -only one through LGPL-2.1 sections 3 and 6 rather than through LGPL-3.0; `scripts/check-licenses.php` already allows both `LGPL-2.1-only` and `LGPL-2.1-or-later`, so the project has treated both forms as AGPL-compatible before.
