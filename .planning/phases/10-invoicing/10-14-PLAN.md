Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-14-1 The "time of the project" linked with a fixed amount is every unbilled, unreserved, finished, billable entry of that project up to now (the draft does not store its creation period); the modal helper names count and duration before the Admin confirms (UI-SPEC C3).
- R-14-2 "Dosud vyfakturováno" for a fixed-price project is the sum of fixed-amount lines of issued invoices of that project minus the credited quantities of those lines, in the project's price currency; amounts in another currency are listed separately, never summed.
- R-14-3 A fixed-amount line is quantity 1, unit `paušál`, origin `fixed_amount` with the project in its source; its unit price is the typed amount in the draft currency and counts as a manual price (no conversion at issue).
