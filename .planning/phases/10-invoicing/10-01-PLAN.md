<objective>
The arithmetic every invoice line needs, before any invoice code exists: a quantity value object with a single, documented rounding of exact seconds to four decimals, and `Money` methods for quantity times price, sign handling, an exact conversion between any two CNB currencies, and the converted line amount computed from one exact rational and rounded once (D-09).

Purpose: IN-02 (hours from exact seconds, editable quantities), IN-07 (conversion at issue), D-02, D-09; research Pattern 5, Pitfall 8, Open Question 6 (resolved by ruling R-01-1 below, not by the research's two-rounding recommendation). Every later plan prices a line through these methods only.
Output: `InvoiceQuantity`, `Money::times()`, `negated()`, `isNegative()`, `isPositive()`, `compareTo()`, `convertThroughCzk()`, `timesThroughCzk()`, two unit test files.

Owner confirmation point (ruling R-01-1, in the manner of plan 10-06 R-06-1): D-09 says the unit price is converted and rounded once per line. This plan rounds the converted line amount once, from the exact conversion of the source price, and prints the converted unit price as a separately rounded display figure. A printed converted line can therefore differ by rounding from quantity x printed unit price (by at most half a minor unit per unit of quantity plus half a minor unit; for example 10 h at a printed 59,70 EUR shows 597,01 EUR, not 597,00 EUR). Ordinary lines always agree exactly. The owner confirms this reading of D-09 before this plan is executed; the alternative the owner may choose instead is the research's two-step rule (round the unit price, then multiply), which agrees on paper but rounds the amount twice and would change rulings R-01-1, R-04-3, R-13-5, R-17-1 and R-23-4.
</objective>

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-01-1 (research Open Question 6 resolved against the research's two-rounding recommendation, to conform to D-09 and Phase 2 D-09): a converted line amount is rounded HALF_UP exactly once. `timesThroughCzk()` computes `minor x quantity x sourceRate x targetUnit / (sourceUnit x targetRate)` (with the fraction-digit scales of both currencies) as one exact rational and hands it to `fromExactMinor()` once. The unit price printed on a converted line is `convertThroughCzk()` of the source unit price: the converted price shown to the customer, rounded once to the target minor unit for display, never multiplied into the line amount. Consequence recorded for the schema (plan 10-04 R-04-3) and the PDF: on a converted line, quantity x printed unit price can differ from the printed line amount by at most half a minor unit per unit of quantity plus half a minor unit; on every other line they agree exactly.
- R-01-2: the quantity is a scaled integer of ten-thousandths. `fromSeconds()` rounds half-up with integer arithmetic (`intdiv(50 x seconds + 9, 18)`), because a quantity is not money and must not pull Brick out of the Money class; this is the one quantity rounding point and its docblock says so.
- R-01-3: `times()` takes the canonical decimal string of a quantity (`InvoiceQuantity::toDecimal()`), so the Shared `Money` class does not depend on the Invoicing domain.
