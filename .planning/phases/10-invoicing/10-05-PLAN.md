<objective>
The two Partner-readable invoice models with their fail-closed Partner scope, their policies and every registry the isolation harness needs.

Purpose: IN-09 (Partner sees own issued documents only), FND-18 harness growth, research Pattern 1 and Pattern 11. Every Admin screen and every Partner screen later reads through these models.

Split note: this plan was split to stay under 15 modified files. The document enums and their labels moved to plan 10-33 (wave 1), the pinned Partner-safe column lists of both tables moved to plan 10-04 (they belong to the table creation). The remaining eleven files cannot be split further without breaking the suite between plans: the enforced morph map, the pinned model declaration list, the activity allowlist list and the canary registry each fail as soon as a model exists without its line, so a model and its registry lines land in the same plan.
