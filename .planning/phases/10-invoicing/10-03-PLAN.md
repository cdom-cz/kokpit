Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-03-1 (research Open Question 4, revised to conform to D-21 and D-23; the research recommended reading logo and signature at render time): accent, footer, logo and signature are frozen into the issue snapshot (plan 10-12), the two images as `{path, sha256}` references to their stored objects, so a regenerated PDF uses exactly the issued inputs. A stored image object is retained when the Admin replaces or clears it in Settings, and `DocumentImage::dataUri()` verifies the SHA-256 before returning the image; a missing or altered object makes the render fail (plans 10-15 and 10-36 report it) instead of printing another image. This overrides UI-SPEC assumption U-8 and the Surface L sentence "Replacing a file removes the old object"; plan 10-21 amends the UI-SPEC text.
- R-03-2: the palette is not stored; it is derived at render time from the snapshotted accent. An invalid stored accent falls back to the default, as the cdom tools rules do.
- R-03-3: logo and signature live on the private media disk (`config('media-library.disk_name')`) under the content-addressed names `settings/document-logo-<sha256>.png` / `settings/document-signature-<sha256>.png` (the SHA-256 of the re-encoded PNG bytes; always re-encoded PNG, a JPEG upload is re-encoded too), dimension cap 4000 x 4000 px, size cap 1 MB. The same image uploaded twice maps to the same object; objects are never deleted by the Settings page.

Analogs (10-PATTERNS.md): settings row "Settings additions (accent, logo, signature, footer)", analogs `app/Domain/Settings/Settings/NumberingSettings.php` and `BankAccountSettings.php` (typed settings with `fieldRules`); `DocumentPalette` is listed under "No Analog Found" (built from the RESEARCH Pattern 9 palette rules); `DocumentImage` follows the hardening of `app/Domain/Documents/Actions/AttachDocuments.php` (finfo, server-generated names) named in that row.
