## Spacing Scale

Inherited from 02, 05, 06 and 08: declared values are multiples of 4, mapped to Filament's Tailwind 4 spacing (1 unit = 4px). Custom markup writes them in rem as 0.25, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4.

| Token | Value | Usage in this phase |
|-------|-------|---------------------|
| xs | 4px | Gap between a badge and its icon, between a value and its secondary line in a cell |
| sm | 8px | Gap between filter controls, between icon and text in a button, between lines of the summary strip |
| md | 16px | Padding of the summary strip and credit-note picker rows, gap between a section heading and its content |
| lg | 24px | Gap between sections of the detail and draft pages (Filament form grid) |
| xl | 32px | Page content horizontal padding, gap between page header and content (Filament stock) |
| 2xl | 48px | Not used on screen |
| 3xl | 64px | Not used on screen |

Component sizes that are not spacing tokens (all multiples of 4): the draft items table has a floor of 960px inside its wrapper, which scrolls sideways on narrow screens while the page does not; columns: index 48px, description flexible with a floor of 320px, quantity 112px, unit 96px, unit price 144px, total 144px, delete 48px; the delete icon button keeps a hit area of at least 24 x 24px with 8px to the next target (WCAG 2.2 AA 2.5.8).

Exceptions and rules:

- Everything inside Filament tables, forms, modals, stats and sections comes from stock components with stock spacing.
- Custom markup uses only the tokens above (F-2: no 0.375rem, no 0.75rem).
- The PDF is a separate medium and declares its own point-based spacing in "Surface H".
- Dashboard band: stock stats behaviour (stacked on a phone, three across from `md`).

## Typography

On screen, inherited from 02, 05, 06 and 08: four sizes and two weights; application code introduces no other.

| Heading (section, widget and modal headings, the summary strip group headings) | 16px (`text-base`) | 600 semibold | 1.5 (24px) |
| Display (page headings stock; the document number on the detail page; the "K úhradě" total and stat values) | 24px below `sm`, 30px from `sm` up for page headings; stat and total values fixed at 30px (`text-3xl`) | 600 semibold | 1.33 / 1.2 |

Contract notes (screen):

- Declared sizes: 14, 16, 24, 30. Declared weights: 400, 600.
- Secondary text (the client under a number, "Po splatnosti o 5 dní", the H:MM under a decimal quantity, the preview-rate note) is Filament's gray text at 14px, never smaller, never below opacity 0.8.
- Money: `Money::format('cs')` on screen (`12 000,00 Kč`, `480,00 €`), `tabular-nums`, right aligned in columns. An invoice amount is always shown in the invoice currency; the CZK equivalent is a labelled second line ("v Kč: :czk"), never mixed into the same cell without a label. Amounts of different currencies are never summed.
- Quantities: decimal with a decimal comma, at most 4 decimals, trailing zeros trimmed (`2,5`, `1,3333`). A line generated from time shows the exact duration as `H:MM` on a gray second line (`DurationFormat::hoursMinutes()`), because the decimal is a rounded display of exact seconds (D-02).
- Dates `j. n. Y`; instants in `Europe/Prague`; empty values `—`.
- Czech diacritics render in Inter on screen. Check "Zálohová faktura", "Částečně zaplaceno", "Po splatnosti", "Stornováno" and "Variabilní symbol" at the gate.

## Access and Visibility Contract

Applies to every new class; the registry and canary tests enforce it (FND-18). Phase 12 proves the whole matrix; this phase must not make it harder.

- Every new Resource, Page, Widget and relation manager declares `#[AccessRule(...)]` with the matching `Enforces*AccessRule` trait. The Admin document resource, all its relation managers, the dashboard widget, the client panels and the settings additions are `Audience::AdminOnly`. The Partner resource is `Audience::PartnerAllowed` and also requires `PartnerContext::partnerClientId() !== null`, repeating the trait conditions like `PartnerProjectResource::canAccess()` so an Admin gets no second navigation entry (403 on the URL).
- The Partner query is the Partner-scoped document model with an extra, explicit rule: only documents of the Partner's own client, only **issued** ones (a draft has no row for a Partner, no count, no empty-state hint), only kinds invoice, proforma and credit note. A document of another client or an archived client is a 404, never a 403.
- The Partner detail and list select only the pinned Partner column builders (a new `InvoiceColumns::partnerColumns()` working name). No column of the following is selected, rendered, put in a Livewire snapshot, an activity view, a notification, the bell or global search: internal note, payments and payment rows, income transaction or category, linked time entries, rate and rate source, CNB rate and CZK total, draft state, e-mail log.
- The issued item lines (description, quantity, unit, unit price, line total) are visible to the Partner because they are printed on the document the client receives (assumption U-3). They are read from the frozen snapshot text copies, never recomputed from time entries or rates.
- Downloads: the invoice PDF goes through the existing signed, short-lived document route with the permission check (DO-03); no public URL. The Partner sees only the PDF of an issued document of their own client.
- The draft editor, the entries tab, every action of Surface E and the settings fields are rendered **only** for the Admin and re-check the Admin on every Livewire update. A hidden action is not rendered, not disabled.
- Hand-written SQL in overdue and dashboard readers is guarded by `PartnerContext` (Admin or system only), because raw joins bypass the Partner scope.
- The canary harness gets one issued invoice and one draft per client, each with an internal-note canary, a payment canary and a linked-time canary. The route walk and a rendered-HTML check prove that the Partner of client A sees none of client B's canary strings, no draft of client A, and none of the strings under "Never on a Partner surface" (Copywriting Contract).

### Surface A: Document list `/admin/invoices` — new (IN-01, IN-05, IN-06, IN-10, D-17, D-18, D-20)

Admin only, heading "Faktury", subheading none. One list holds invoices, proformas and credit notes, drafts included; the kind is a column and a filter (they share the screen, not the number series).

- **Header actions:** "Nová faktura" (primary, `OutlinedPlus`) opens Surface B. Nothing else in the header.
- **Tabs** (`Tab` with count badge; the count is computed over the whole table, not the page): "Všechny" (default), "Koncepty", "Nezaplacené" (issued, not fully paid, not overdue), "Po splatnosti" (`danger` badge), "Zaplacené", "Stornované", and "Zálohy k vyfakturování" (paid proformas without an invoice, `danger` badge). The last tab is **absent when its count is zero** (no empty tab, no placeholder), which is how the D-18 attention item stays quiet when nothing is wrong. A credit note appears under "Všechny" only; it has no payment state.
- **Columns** in order: Číslo (semibold; a draft has no number and shows `—`; links to the edit page for a draft, to the view page otherwise), Druh (gray badge), Klient (link to the client), Vystaveno (`j. n. Y`; `—` for a draft), Splatnost (`j. n. Y`; for an overdue row the date in `danger` text and a second gray line "Po splatnosti o :count dní", plural per Czech rules), Celkem (invoice currency), K úhradě (invoice currency; `—` for a draft, a credit note, a paid or cancelled document; partial payments show the remaining amount), Stav (badge, see colour map; an overdue partially paid row adds a gray second line "Částečně zaplaceno"), E-mail (icon column with tooltip: `OutlinedEnvelope` success "Odesláno :date", `OutlinedClock` info "Ve frontě", `OutlinedExclamationCircle` danger "Odeslání selhalo", `—` otherwise; credit notes and proformas included; drafts show nothing). Toggleable and hidden by default: Měna, Vytvořeno, Variabilní symbol.
- **Sorting:** default for "Všechny": drafts first by creation time descending, then issued documents by issue date descending, then number descending. "Po splatnosti" sorts by due date ascending (the oldest overdue first). "Zálohy k vyfakturování" sorts by payment date ascending. 25 rows per page, options 25, 50, 100.
- **"Po splatnosti" overview (D-20):** the same table, plus a table summarizer under "K úhradě": "Celkem v Kč" (remaining amount converted with the frozen issue-time CNB rate for foreign-currency documents, each converted once, summed in CZK only). Foreign-currency rows show "v Kč: :czk" as a gray second line under "K úhradě". The overdue test is the derived rule of D-20 (issued, not fully paid, due date before today in Europe/Prague).
- **Filters** (AND, indicator chips): Druh (select), Klient (searchable select, archived clients included and marked "(archivovaný)"), Vystaveno (Od, Do), Měna, E-mail (Odesláno, Ve frontě, Selhalo, Neodesláno; this is the link target of the System page slot).

The customer column prints the customer's address and IDs from the snapshot.
- **Date strip:** marginTop 24pt, a 0.75pt hairline above and below, three equal cells with a hairline between: "Datum vystavení" / "Issue date", "Datum zdanitelného plnění" / "Taxable supply date" (**only in VAT-payer mode**, so non-payer documents show two cells: issue and due), "Datum splatnosti" / "Due date". Columns: index 32pt (centred, blank header), description (flexible, 8pt right padding, blank header), "Množství" / "Qty" 60pt right (number plus unit, for example `2,5 h`), "Jednotková cena" / "Unit price" 84pt right, "Celkem" / "Total" 88pt right. In VAT-payer mode a "DPH %" column of 40pt is added (data model only in v1, no template logic). Rows: padding 8pt vertical, odd rows on the row tint, descriptions wrap, the row is not split. Quantity uses the document's number format (cs `2,5`, en `2.5`).
- **Summary:** right-aligned block marginTop 16pt, not split from the table's last row. In non-VAT-payer mode it is exactly one element: the total bar (accent background, on-accent text, padding 12pt vertical and 16pt horizontal, minimum width 224pt): label 8pt bold letter-spaced 1.2pt uppercase ("K ÚHRADĚ" / "AMOUNT DUE"; credit note "DOBROPIS CELKEM" / "CREDIT TOTAL"), amount 15pt bold. Left: the QR in a 96pt white square with 4pt padding, **only** when the document currency is CZK, the bank account is a CZ account with an IBAN, and the kind is invoice or proforma. Right: the sentence "Prosíme o úhradu částky :amount na účet :account s variabilním symbolem :vs do :due." / "Please pay :amount to account :account with variable symbol :vs by :due." (amount, account, symbol and date bold), then a two-column fact list (8pt, keys muted 64pt wide): "Číslo účtu", "IBAN", "BIC" (each only if present), "Variabilní symbol", "Způsob úhrady" ("Převodem"), "K úhradě". Případné vrácení platby proběhne samostatně." / "This credit note carries no payment details. Any refund is made separately."
- **QR content (SPAYD 1.0):** `SPD*1.0*ACC:<IBAN without spaces>*AM:<amount with two decimals>*CC:CZK*X-VS:<variable symbol, digits, at most 10>*DT:<YYYYMMDD due date>*MSG:<message, ASCII, diacritics stripped, `*` replaced by a space, at most 60 characters>`. Generated locally (no remote request) as a PNG data URI with error correction level M and a quiet zone of 4 modules. The message is the document number in the form "Faktura :number".
- **Optional blocks:** the free note "Poznámka na dokladu" (8pt muted, above the payment box), the supplier footer text (8pt muted, marginTop 16pt, from settings), the signature image (marginTop 16pt, right aligned, max height 80pt, max width 200pt, contained; omitted when none is set).
- **Fixed furniture on every page:** a 0.75pt hairline rule 44pt above the bottom edge between the margins, and "Strana :n / :total" / "Page :n / :total" at 8pt muted, right aligned, 24pt above the bottom edge. (The reference prints `n/total`; the existing work report prints "Strana n / total", and this keeps the Kokpit wording, assumption U-9.)
- **Variants:** invoice, proforma ("ZÁLOHOVÁ FAKTURA", the heading line "Nejde o daňový doklad." / "This is not a tax document." under the party blocks, and the source of the later invoice is not printed), credit note (negative amounts with a minus, "Opravuje doklad", no payment box). - **Safety:** all data is escaped by Blade `{{ }}`; images are local data URIs; no remote asset; item text cannot inject markup. The logo and signature accept PNG and JPEG only (no SVG), at most 1 MB each (Surface L).
- **Failure:** a render failure at issue rolls the whole issue back (D-23, Surface D toast). A render failure on a later regeneration shows the danger toast "PDF se nepodařilo vytvořit" with the body "Zkuste to znovu. Pokud chyba přetrvá, zkontrolujte úložiště souborů." and keeps the existing file untouched.

### Cross-cutting interaction rules (this phase)

- Keyboard: every control works by keyboard alone with the accent focus ring: the tabs, the item table cells and the drag-handle alternatives, the toggle buttons, modals (Esc closes, Enter submits, focus returns to the trigger), the row actions.
- A hidden action is not rendered, not disabled (a payment on a credit note, "Vystavit fakturu" on an unpaid proforma, every Admin action for a Partner).
- Issuing, storno, credit notes and payments are single transactions: any failure rolls the whole step back and shows a danger toast with a next step and no exception text.
- Destructive actions use the stock confirmation modal (`requiresConfirmation` styling) with the action name as the submit label; the only destructive-coloured controls are listed under Color.
- Money never appears next to a value of a different currency in one cell; a CZK-only figure carries "v Kč" or the column title.
- Enum labels (document kind, payment state, payment method, e-mail state) come from `lang/cs/enums.php`; new strings go to `lang/cs/invoices.php` (screen) and `lang/{cs,en}/invoice_pdf.php`, `lang/{cs,en}/invoice_mail.php` (documents and e-mails), following the `work_report.php` precedent.
- Every list that can grow polls nothing by default; the document list refreshes on the Livewire events `invoice-payment-saved` and `invoice-email-state-changed` only.
