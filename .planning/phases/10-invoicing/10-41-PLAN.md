Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-41-1 (D-19 as amended; research Open Question 7, adopted) `DeletePayment::handle(User $actor, InvoicePayment $payment): void` is the only deletion path of a payment and of payment income. Order: `Gate::forUser($actor)->authorize('delete', $payment)` (AdminOnlyPolicy of plan 10-08) before the transaction; then, inside one `DB::transaction`: lock the document row (`Invoice` by the payment's `invoice_id`, `lockForUpdate`, a row lock fires no UPDATE trigger, so the frozen guard is not touched); re-read the payment under `lockForUpdate` (gone: `ValidationException` `payment_missing` under `payment`); refuse a payment that is the source of a carried copy (R-41-2); lock the income (`Transaction` by `invoice_payment_id`, null on a proforma payment); `set_config('kokpit.payment_deletion', <payment id>, true)`; delete each receipt of the income through `DeleteDocument` ordered by id; delete the income through the model (its `deleted` activity); delete the payment through the model (its `deleted` activity); clear the flag with `set_config('kokpit.payment_deletion', '', true)` so it never outlives the sanctioned statements, even inside a caller's or a test's wrapping transaction. The income goes first because `transactions_invoice_payment_fk` is NO ACTION; the deferred pair check of plan 10-06 R-06-7 then finds the payment gone at commit. Nothing is written on `invoices`; statuses, remaining amounts, overdue and the attention item are derived (`InvoiceBalances`, `ReceivablesReader`).
- R-41-2 (IN-06, D-15, D-18) Carried payments: a carried copy on the invoice is an ordinary invoice payment and is deleted with its income; its proforma source is never touched. A proforma payment that is the source of a carried copy (an `invoice_payments` row with `carried_from_payment_id` naming it exists) is refused with `payment_delete_carried` (under `payment`, `:number` the invoice number of the copy) while that copy exists, before the flag is set; the self-referencing key of plan 10-06 R-06-1 refuses the same at the database (23503). Once the copy is gone the source is deletable like any proforma payment (no income exists on a proforma). Proforma status "Převedeno na fakturu" follows the conversion link and does not change; an unconverted proforma whose payments no longer cover its total stops being "Zaplacená záloha bez faktury". This deviates from the research recommendation (refuse every payment of a converted proforma) because the owner amendment makes every mistaken payment correctable.
- R-41-3 Lock order: the document row, then the payment row, then the income row, the same document-first order as `RecordPayment` (plan 10-16) and `ConvertProforma` (plan 10-22 R-22-1, which locks the proforma). A deletion of a proforma payment and a conversion of that proforma therefore serialise on the proforma row: whichever commits second re-checks under the lock and refuses with its typed message; two deletions of the same payment serialise and the second finds the row gone.
- R-41-4 Scope: any issued invoice or proforma, a cancelled invoice included (D-16 storno does not touch payments; the Admin returns money manually, and a mistaken payment stays correctable); a draft or a credit note has no payments (plan 10-06 insert guard). There is no payment edit. The activity log records the deletion through the models' `LogsAllowlistedActivity` (`deleted` event, allowlisted attributes: invoice, amount, currency, date, method, carried link; never the note); no bell notice and no e-mail. Phase 11 decides whether an online payment matched from Stripe may use this path (hand-over in CONTRIBUTING, plan 10-32).
- R-41-5 The flag literal `kokpit.payment_deletion` appears in `app/` only in `DeletePayment.php` and in `database/migrations/` only in `2026_10_17_000300_create_invoice_admin_tables.php` and `2026_10_17_000400_link_transactions_to_invoices.php`; `tests/Arch/PaymentDeletionPathTest.php` scans both directories and fails on any other file (tests may use the literal to prove the guards).
