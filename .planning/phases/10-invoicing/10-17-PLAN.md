Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-17-1 (research Open Question 6 resolved against the research's two-rounding recommendation, to conform to D-09 and Phase 2 D-09, as plan 10-01 R-01-1): one rounding per converted line. The line amount is `Money::timesThroughCzk(quantity, quotes of the issue date)` of the source rate, rounded HALF_UP once; the printed unit price is `Money::convertThroughCzk()` of the source rate, rounded once for display and never multiplied into the amount; `price_converted` true lets the database bound the difference (plan 10-04 R-04-3). On a converted line, quantity x printed unit price can differ from the printed line amount by less than half a minor unit per unit of quantity plus half a minor unit; this is the cost of rounding the amount once, and the UI-SPEC amendment of plan 10-21 records it.
- R-17-2 `conversion_snapshot` shape `{source_currency, source_rate, source_unit, target_currency, target_rate, target_unit, rate_date}`; the CZK side is rate `1`, unit 1, and its `rate_date` is the later of the two list dates.
- R-17-3 Payer mode blocks issuing (truth above); the blocker key is `vat_payer_unsupported`, added to `lang/cs/invoices.php` errors.
