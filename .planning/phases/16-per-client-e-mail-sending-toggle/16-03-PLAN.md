<objective>
Give the document e-mail a visible "not sent, by the client's switch" outcome: a new `skipped` state, written by the send job when it finds the client switched off after its claim or immediately before the transport call (or when it gives up a stale row of such a client), read everywhere as "not sent" rather than "failed", and quiet on the System page. Hide the resend in the document list row and the Odeslání row for a hand-served client.

Purpose: P16-05 (document e-mail half: re-check after the claim and right before the transport call, skipped, no lock, no revive), P16-06 (skipped is neither failed nor unsent; the indicator ignores hand-served clients), P16-07 (list row and Odeslání row half); D-04 (list and tab), D-06 (job), D-07, D-08; owner answer A3.
Output: one migration, the enum case and label, the job changes, the list, tab and indicator changes, tests.
</objective>

The class docblock gains a paragraph on the switch (Phase 16, D-06, D-07): the switch is read fresh twice, once after the claim and once as the last statement before the transport call, and a row whose client is switched off at either read ends skipped, unsent and unlocked.
