<objective>
Give the cash receipt (PPD) the same hand-lock as the invoice: the Admin closes an open receipt from the Platby tab without a reason, the lock is audited and confined by the architecture test, and a later confirmation send never locks it a second time. This is the only way to close the receipt of a hand-served cash payment, whose confirmation is skipped (research Pitfall 8).

Purpose: P16-09 (manual PPD lock), P16-10 (unlock unchanged, receipt lock literal confined, guard unchanged); D-02, D-03; owner answer to research open question 3 (the PPD lock lives on the Platby tab only).
Output: `LockCashReceipt`, `CashReceiptAudit::lockedManually()`, the Platby tab action, the extended architecture test, Czech copy, tests.
</objective>
