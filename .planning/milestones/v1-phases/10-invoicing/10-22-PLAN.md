Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R-22-1 Order inside the transaction: lock the proforma (the document row first, the lock order shared with `RecordPayment` and `DeletePayment`, plan 10-41 R-41-3, so a deletion of a proforma payment racing the conversion serialises on this lock), check it, create the invoice draft with copied lines (copied unit prices are marked as manual so the issue does not convert them again), issue it through `DocumentIssuer` (no entries are linked to it yet, so its billing step bills nothing), repoint the proforma's entries to it, carry the payments with income. Repointing after the issue keeps the link guard rule "billed with a link only for an issued document" and the strict billing step both satisfied.
- R-22-2 The issue date of the converted invoice is today; its due date is today plus the client's terms (it is already paid, D-11 defaults apply); its CZK figures use the CNB list of its own issue date.
- R-22-3 The converted invoice's printed note is copied from the proforma; the internal note is not.
