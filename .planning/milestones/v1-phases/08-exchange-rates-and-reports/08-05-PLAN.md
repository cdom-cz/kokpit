<objective>
The conversion helper every later phase uses: a rate book that answers "which CNB rate applies to this currency on this day" (previous valid list on weekends and holidays, by lookup), a converter that returns CZK amounts through it, and the two exact `Money` methods the reports need so every amount is rounded exactly once.

Purpose: EX-03 (conversion helper for invoice and transaction CZK amounts; Phases 9 and 10 reuse it, and the document snapshot is theirs), and the money building blocks of RE-02 (CZK per entry at the entry's day) and RE-05 (effective hourly rate). UI-SPEC U-7: the helper is a service only, no calculator UI.
Output: `RateBook`, `ExchangeRateConverter`, `ExchangeRateMissing`, `Money::forDurationConverted()`, `Money::hourlyRateFor()`, tests.
</objective>

Decisions carried in (locked): D-01 (rate of the day, previous valid rate on weekends and holidays), D-02 (per-unit amounts handled in the conversion helper), D-04 (the last known rate keeps being used until a new one arrives), D-05 (conversion helper returns the CZK amount for a currency, amount and date). Decisions made in this plan (recorded, Claude's discretion in CONTEXT.md):
- Days without a list are resolved by lookup (newest `rate_date <= day` per currency), never stored as copies (research Pattern 3).
- No rate at all for a currency and day throws `ExchangeRateMissing` (currency and day only); callers show a visible marker, never zero.
- Staleness is the health slot's job (plan 08-10), not the converter's: the newest earlier list is used however old.
- The converter adds no own role check: `ExchangeRate` is fail-closed for Partners, so a Partner context finds no rate and gets `ExchangeRateMissing` (test proves it); rates are not client data.
- New `Money` methods take decimal rate strings and integers only and call `fromExactMinor()` once.

Fictional data only: every rate in the tests is invented. Run `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
