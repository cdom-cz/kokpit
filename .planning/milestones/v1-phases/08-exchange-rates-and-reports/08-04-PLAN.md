<objective>
The thinnest end-to-end path of the rates feature: the console command fetches today's CNB list over HTTP, validates it field by field with rates kept as decimal strings, and stores the whole list idempotently in `exchange_rates`; plus the rates area's reviewed contract (timetable and limits in `config/kokpit.php`, the complete Czech copy in `lang/cs/kokpit.php`).

Purpose: EX-01 (idempotent download, per-unit amounts) and D-02 (whole list stored); the contract part serves EX-02 and D-03 to D-05. Later rate plans (schedule and alert, health slot, gap heal, overview, backfill) build on this client, this Action and this contract.
Output: `CnbClient` with its value and failure types, `DownloadCnbRates`, `kokpit:rates:download`, invented CNB fixtures, the `rates` config block, the rates copy, tests with the refusal matrix and the mutation self-check.
</objective>

Decisions made in this plan (recorded):
- The list is fetched from `/exrates/daily` with `lang=EN` and stored under the payload's `validFor`, never under the requested day (research Pattern 1, Pitfall 3). Rate values are quoted in the raw body before `json_decode` so they stay decimal strings (Pitfall 2). A stored list is never rewritten (Pattern 2).
- `CNB_API_BASE_URL` has a default in `config/services.php` and, like `ARES_BASE_URL`, is not listed in `.env.example` (08-VALIDATION.md: EnvExampleTest documents only keys read without a default).
- Command output names dates, counts and the Czech reason only.
- Why the whole rates copy and config land here: plans of other areas edit `lang/cs/kokpit.php` and `config/kokpit.php` in waves 3 to 5, and same-wave plans may not share a file; putting the area's reviewed contract (exact UI-SPEC copy, D-03 to D-05 values) in the area's first plan lets the later rate plans run in parallel waves without touching either file. - Console and failure-reason copy is not a UI-SPEC surface; it is planner-written Czech in the same tone (formal, no exclamation marks). The alert body for "no rate stored yet" (`alert.body_none`) is also planner-written, because the contract body names a last known date that may not exist.

Fictional data only: every CNB payload in tests is built at runtime by `Tests\Support\CnbFixtures` with invented rates; no real list is copied. Run `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
