Uses from plans 08-05, 08-09 and 08-14: `Money::hourlyRateFor()`, `ExchangeRateConverter::toCzk()`, `ExchangeRateMissing`, `ReportFilters` (`to`), `TimeReport::build()` with the project nodes and their `fixed` flag, the tree view with the "Pevná cena" badge and the "Chybí kurz" badge, the copy `kokpit.report.effective.*`.

Decisions carried in (locked): D-07 (fixed-price projects show hours, the agreed fixed price and the effective hourly rate; no per-entry amount; reversibility costly), D-08 (effective hourly rate = fixed price / all worked hours of the whole project, not only the period, in client currency and CZK, with the price and the hours next to it). UI-SPEC Surface B4, U-3 (conversion day: last day of the selected period capped at today; confirmed by the owner 2026-10-14).

Decisions made in this plan (recorded):
- Assumption A3: "all worked hours of the whole project" = every entry of the project, billable or not, finished or running, at any date (the figure of `TimeTotals::forProject()['worked']`).
- The CZK effective rate is derived from the converted price (`Money::hourlyRateFor($priceCzk, $seconds)`), each figure rounded once.
- The block lists every project whose `project_billing.billing_type` is `fixed_price` and that appears in the tree; whole-project seconds come from one grouped query (PHP clock bound to `elapsedSecondsSql`) and prices from one query.
- A CZK project needs no conversion: the CZK lines and the rate-day line are omitted.

Fictional data only. Run `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
