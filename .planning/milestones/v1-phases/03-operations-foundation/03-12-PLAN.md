Let the Admin read the audit trail in two places: a global, filterable, read-only activity overview and a reusable read-only history relation manager that later phases attach to their records with five lines; keep both invisible to Partners and close the boot-order gap for relation managers and widgets (D-07, D-08 labels, Pitfall 1).

Purpose: FND-08 is only useful if the Admin can see the recorded changes; later phases (tasks, projects, invoices, time entries) attach the relation manager instead of building their own history view.
Output: `ActivityResource` with its list page, `ActivityPresenter`, the abstract relation manager, two trait boot hooks, a test fixture and tests.
</objective>
