<objective>
Complete the scalar settings groups on the settings page: VAT mode and payment due days on the invoicing tab and the online-payment toggle on its own tab, each validated in the form and again at the data layer, saved together in one transaction (D-02; FND-07 VAT mode, payment terms, online-payment toggle).

Purpose: invoicing (Phase 10) and Stripe (Phase 11) read these values; an unsupported VAT mode or a half-saved settings page would corrupt every later invoice.
Output: `VatMode`, `InvoicingSettings` and `PaymentSettings` with migrations, the invoicing and online payments tabs, tests.
</objective>
