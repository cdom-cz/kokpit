<objective>
Give the Admin one System page that renders a registry of six health indicators behind one interface, with a test that fails when a slot is missing, a fail-safe result for a throwing indicator, and a page that a Partner can never open (D-12, D-13).

Purpose: FND-10. Phases 8, 10 and 11 fill the rate-date, unsent-e-mail and webhook slots through `HealthIndicatorRegistry::replace()`; plan 03-16 registers the real failed-jobs, oldest-pending and scheduler indicators. FND-10 is partially delivered by design in Phase 3: the last rate date, unprocessed webhooks and unsent invoice e-mails indicators stay placeholders until their phases.
Output: health domain classes, the placeholder indicator, the registry binding, the System page and its view, tests.
</objective>
