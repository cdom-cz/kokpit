<objective>
Make the Phase 10 payment write path callable by a queue job that has no user, without opening any new way to write a payment or its income: the body of `RecordPayment::handle()` becomes `recordLocked()`, the income goes through a link-only `RecordTransaction::writePaymentIncome()`, and the database accepts a card payment with no creator and nothing else without one.

Purpose: PA-06 (a matched Stripe payment creates the payment and the income), PA-08 (the "assign to a document" resolution uses the same core); CONTEXT carried rule (a matched payment is written only into `invoice_payments` and its income only through the rules of `RecordPayment`); D-10, D-13, D-14; research Pattern 6 and Pitfall 5; UI-SPEC Surface G.
Output: the migration, `PaymentMethod::Card`, `PaymentFacts`, the split of `RecordPayment` and `RecordTransaction`, the modal and Platby-tab changes, the feature test.
</objective>

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R11-02-1 (research Pattern 6, Pitfall 5): `RecordPayment::recordLocked(Invoice $locked, PaymentFacts $facts, ?User $actor): InvoicePayment` holds today's body after the lock unchanged: the state check (`payment_not_allowed`), the CNB-list check for the income date, the insert with `forceFill`, and `bookIncome()` for an invoice. It throws `LogicException` outside a transaction (the `carry()` guard) and when `$actor` is null with a method other than card. `created_by` is `$actor?->getKey()`. `handle()` keeps the Gate, the transaction and the lock and calls `recordLocked()` with `PaymentInput::toFacts()`.
- R11-02-2: `RecordTransaction::writePaymentIncome(array $data, array $invoiceLink): Transaction` is the body of `handle()` after the Gate, typed so the invoice link (`invoice_id`, `invoice_payment_id`) is required; `handle()` stays `Gate` then the shared body. `bookIncome()` of RecordPayment calls `writePaymentIncome()` for every payment, so the manual path is authorised once by `RecordPayment::handle()` (update on the invoice), as today.
- R11-02-3 (UI-SPEC Surface G and M): the income description of a card payment is `invoices.payment_income.card_description` "Platba kartou faktury :number"; transfer and cash keep "Platba faktury :number".
- R11-02-4 (migration): `invoice_payments_method_check` becomes `method IN ('transfer', 'cash', 'card')`; `created_by` drops NOT NULL; `invoice_payments_creator_check CHECK (method = 'card' OR created_by IS NOT NULL)`. A card payment assigned by the Admin (plan 11-19) carries the Admin as creator; one from the webhook carries none. DDL fires no row guard; `down()` refuses to run while a card payment exists (exception naming the reason) and otherwise restores both constraints and NOT NULL.
- R11-02-5: `PaymentMethod::Card` ("Kartou") is never selectable by hand: `PaymentMethod::manual()` returns Transfer and Cash, the modal lists only them, and `PaymentInput::method()` refuses card under `method` with `invoices.errors.payment_method`.

<assumption_delta_decision>
- Detector: `assumption-delta scan 11` detected pluralization ("second payment", "link id as fallback").
- Primary noun: the payment of a document (`invoice_payments`), whatever the route. A card payment is a variant of it (method card); who created it becomes optional for that variant only.
- Decision: promote. The "every payment has a user" assumption is generalised to "every manual payment has a user" (R11-02-4) instead of adding a second payments table alongside; the Stripe facts live in the separate event record of plan 11-09 and point at the payment.
- Invariant test (companion, accepted): for every PaymentMethod case and for an invoice and a proforma, one recorded payment books exactly one income on an invoice and none on a proforma, through the one core (Task 2).
</assumption_delta_decision>
