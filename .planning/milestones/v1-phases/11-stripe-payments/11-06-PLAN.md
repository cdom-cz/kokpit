<objective>
Give the payment link of a document a record of its own: an Admin-only `payment_links` table whose database rules make a wrong link impossible to store (wrong kind, wrong currency, two live links, a changed amount, a deleted record).

Purpose: PA-01 and PA-04 (the state machine the link job, the reconcile service, the pay page and the link section read and write); D-05, D-06, D-07, D-08; research "Schema" item 2, Pitfall 13.
Output: the migration with its guard trigger, the model, the two enums with Czech labels, the registrations (morph alias, policy, model declaration, activity allowlist, canary fixture), the feature test.
</objective>

Rulings made in this plan (recorded):
- R11-06-1 (research Schema item 2): columns `id` uuid v7; `invoice_id`, `currency` char(3) with `payment_links_invoice_currency_fk FOREIGN KEY (invoice_id, currency) REFERENCES invoices (id, currency)`; `amount_minor bigint` (`payment_links_amount_check > 0`); `state varchar(16) DEFAULT 'pending'` (`payment_links_state_check`: pending, active, failed, deactivated); `deactivation_reason varchar(16)` (`payment_links_deactivation_check`: in paid, cancelled, amount_changed, manual, replaced, and present exactly when deactivated); `failure_class varchar(32)` (`payment_links_failure_check`: present exactly when failed); `stripe_link_id varchar(255)` unique (`payment_links_stripe_link_id_unique`); `stripe_url varchar(500)` (`payment_links_stripe_url_check`: null or `^https://([a-z0-9-]+\.)*stripe\.com/`); `payment_links_active_check` (active needs both Stripe columns); `activated_at`, `deactivated_at`, `remote_deactivated_at` timestamptz (`payment_links_remote_check`: remote only on a deactivated row); `created_by` nullable foreign key to users; `timestampsTz`. Indexes: partial unique `payment_links_one_live_per_invoice ON (invoice_id) WHERE state IN ('pending', 'active')`; `payment_links_remote_pending_index ON (deactivated_at) WHERE state = 'deactivated' AND stripe_link_id IS NOT NULL AND remote_deactivated_at IS NULL` (the sweep of plan 11-10); `payment_links_invoice_created_index ON (invoice_id, created_at)`.
- R11-06-2 (defence in depth, D-06, D-08): trigger function `kokpit_guard_payment_link()` BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OR DELETE, SQLSTATE KP001: an INSERT needs the invoice issued, not a credit note (read `FOR SHARE`) and its `invoice_admin.card_payment_enabled` true; a DELETE is refused; an UPDATE may not change `invoice_id`, `currency`, `amount_minor`, `created_by`, `created_at`; allowed state moves are pending to active, failed or deactivated, and active to deactivated; on a failed or deactivated row only `stripe_link_id` and `stripe_url` may go from null to a value, `remote_deactivated_at` from null to a value, and `updated_at` change; a set `stripe_link_id` never changes. Plus `Immutability::truncateGuardSql('payment_links')`.
- R11-06-3: `PaymentLink extends KokpitModel implements PartnerIsolated` with `DeniesPartners`, `LogsAllowlistedActivity`, `#[LoggedAttributes(['invoice_id', 'currency', 'amount_minor', 'state', 'deactivation_reason', 'failure_class', 'activated_at', 'deactivated_at', 'remote_deactivated_at'])]` (no Stripe reference); nothing fillable; casts to the enums, integer and `CarbonImmutable`; `amount(): Money`; `isLive(): bool`; scope `live()`; `open(Invoice $lockedInvoice, int $amountMinor, ?User $by): self` (LogicException outside a transaction; a pending row in the invoice's currency); `latestFor(string $invoiceId): ?self` (the live row first, otherwise the newest). Morph alias `payment_link`; `Gate::policy(PaymentLink::class, AdminOnlyPolicy::class)`.
- R11-06-4 (UI-SPEC colour map and copy): labels in `lang/cs/enums.php` under `payment_link_state` ("Připravuje se", "Aktivní", "Vytvoření selhalo", "Deaktivován") and `payment_link_deactivation` ("Doklad je zaplacený.", "Doklad byl stornován.", "Po částečné platbě se nabízená částka změnila.", "Deaktivován ručně.", "Nahrazen novým odkazem."). "Vypnuto" is not a state; plan 11-12 derives it from the frozen switch.
