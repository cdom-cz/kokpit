Assumption adopted (flagged for owner confirmation):
- A5: "relevant changes" are status, priority and assignee; dates, title and description changes do not notify. One notification per save lists every changed field (coalesced).
- A13: an assignee who can receive the escalation but switched the escalation event off on both channels (D-15) gets nothing, and the Admin is not notified in that assignee's place; the escalation stays visible as the flag on the board card and the task page. This is the planner's reading of how D-07 "falling back to the Admin" meets D-15, not an owner decision: D-15 says the preferences only narrow delivery, and an Admin fallback triggered by somebody else's preference would widen the Admin's delivery, so the default keeps the fallback for an assignee who cannot receive (below). The item is listed for owner confirmation in the 05-17 SUMMARY. If the owner wants the Admin notified in that case, the change is one branch in `TaskNotifier::escalated` (read `NotificationPreferences::for($assignee)` for both channels at dispatch and pick the Admin when both are off) plus one test case; the Admin's own preferences still apply, and an escalation comment is never internal, so the internal-comment rule is unaffected.

Locked decision applied (not an assumption): D-07 "The escalation flag notifies the assignee, falling back to the Admin." Research Open Question 3 is resolved to follow it. The recipient is the assignee, including an active Partner assignee (who may clear the flag per D-06, plan 05-13). The Admin is the fallback only when the assignee cannot receive it: a deactivated account, a Partner of an archived client, a Partner whose `client_id` is not the project's client, or the escalating Partner (an actor is never notified of the own action, as for comments in plan 05-15). The availability check reuses the one `TaskNotifier` applies to comment recipients (plan 05-15: `deactivated_at`, archived client) and adds the client match for a Partner assignee. Preferences (D-15) are read at send time in `via()` and only narrow the chosen recipient's channels; that they never move the notification to the Admin is assumption A13 above, not part of the locked decision. The unconditional rule that internal comments never reach a Partner is untouched: an escalation comment is never internal (`task_comments_internal_escalation_check`), and the internal guard of `TaskNotification` stays in force.

Fictional data only; canaries assembled at runtime. Run `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
