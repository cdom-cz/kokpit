<objective>
Close the panel side of Partner isolation: every Filament class must declare an access rule that also drives its behaviour (D-03), the stock dashboard and widgets give way to a declared dashboard, Resources run under strict authorization, and the canary harness grows a registry and a route walk that requests every panel page as a Partner and searches the responses for the other client's canary strings (D-04).

Purpose: FND-18 and the panel half of FND-06. Filament Pages and Widgets default to visible for every signed-in user, and strict authorization covers Resources only (RESEARCH Pattern 5), so without an enforced declaration a later screen leaks by default.
Output: `AccessRule`, `Audience`, `AccessRules`, four enforcement traits, `Dashboard`, panel changes, a test-only canary Resource, the canary registry and four test files.
</objective>
