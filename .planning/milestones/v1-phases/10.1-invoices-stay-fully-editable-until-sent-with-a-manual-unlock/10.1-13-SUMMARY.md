# Phase 10.1 Plan 13: Card payments follow the current document currency Summary

**A paid Stripe session of an old-currency link is recorded as unmatched `currency` instead of being booked in the document's new currency, and a database trigger (KP001) pins every card payment record to a payment in its own currency (review CR-01).**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 25 min
- **Completed:** 2026-10-23
- **Tasks:** 2 (1 tracer, 1 auto/tdd)
- **Files:** 4 (1 created, 3 modified)

## Accomplishments

- `RecordCardPayment::handle()` step 5 now records unmatched `currency` when the paid currency differs from the link's currency or the link's currency differs from the locked document's current currency. The class docblock item 5 names the document comparison and why (CR-01) and points at the database backstop.
- Webhook regression through the signed endpoint: an issued CZK card invoice of 12 500,00 is switched to EUR with the same figure through `UpdateIssuedDocument`, the old CZK session is paid, and the result is one `unmatched`/`currency` record (paid and offered currency CZK, 1 250 000), no `invoice_payments` row, no income, remaining 1 250 000, the invoices row unchanged. Positive control: the paid session of the new EUR link is matched, one payment, one income in EUR, remaining 0.
- Migration `2026_10_23_000100_pin_card_payment_currency_to_its_payment.php`: `kokpit_guard_card_payment_currency()` and trigger `card_payments_currency_guard BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OF invoice_payment_id`, KP001 "a card payment record points only at a payment in the currency it was paid in". Reversible `down()`.
- Raw-SQL tests: insert of a EUR record pointing at a CZK payment refused, fill of a stored EUR record with a CZK payment refused, same-currency fill and clearing allowed, an unrelated update of a matched record allowed.
