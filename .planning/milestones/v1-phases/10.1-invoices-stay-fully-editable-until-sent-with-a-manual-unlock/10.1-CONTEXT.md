# Phase 10.1: Invoices stay fully editable (items and details) until sent, with a manual unlock (INSERTED) - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-22
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

An issued invoice (and proforma) is no longer frozen at issue. It stays fully editable (items, dates, customer and supplier data, currency, language, linked time) until it is **successfully e-mailed**; from then on it is locked, and Admin can **unlock it manually with a recorded reason**. Payment state and credit notes do **not** lock anything.

This deliberately **reverses** Phase 10 D-13 (DB guard triggers freeze every issued row), the "issued invoice is never edited" half of D-14, and the Immutability constraint in `.planning/PROJECT.md` ("issued invoices ... are immutable"). The roadmap title ("until paid or credited") is superseded by this discussion: the lock is **send-driven, not status-driven**. PROJECT.md, REQUIREMENTS.md (IN-03) and the roadmap goal must be amended accordingly.

Out of scope: new document kinds, refund automation, Partner view changes beyond what the new edit/lock behaviour forces.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Lock model
- **D-01:** An issued invoice or proforma is locked **only by a successfully sent e-mail** (the e-mail reaches the sent state in `invoice_emails` / `InvoiceEmailState`). A queued, pending or failed e-mail does not lock. Payments (manual or card), partial payments, credit notes and storno do **not** lock and are not lock conditions; the payment state is simply recomputed from the data. — **Reversibility:** costly — replaces the status-driven immutability that DB guards, actions, policies and the Partner view were built on.
- **D-02:** No "mark as sent manually" action in this phase; only an actual successful e-mail locks.
- **D-03:** Admin can **unlock** a locked invoice with an `Unlock` action that requires a **reason**; the unlock is audited. The invoice stays editable until the next successful e-mail send locks it again. There is no one-edit auto-relock.
- **D-04:** Editing an invoice that has payments or credit notes is allowed (when unlocked); the system shows a warning when totals fall below what was paid or credited and recomputes the payment status. No hard block.

### What stays editable (while unlocked)
- **D-05:** Items (text, quantity, unit, price, add, delete) are editable; line amounts and totals are recomputed with the same single `HALF_UP` rounding as at issue.
- **D-06:** Dates (issue, taxable supply, due), customer and supplier snapshots and the bank account, and invoice currency and language are editable. A currency change re-takes the CNB rate and CZK total (Phase 8 `ExchangeRateConverter`) and the bank account follows the currency (Phase 3 D-04). **Owner ruling (2026-10-23), amends D-04 for this one field:** the currency of a document that already has a payment or a credit note cannot be changed (money received is not re-denominated); every other edit of such a document stays allowed with a warning.
- **D-07:** Linked time can be added and released on an issued invoice exactly as on a draft (Phase 10 D-03, D-05, D-06): adding generates new items, releasing returns entries to unbilled. While the invoice is locked the time stays locked with it; unlocking frees it for these changes. Time entry billing state and the entry-to-invoice link guard must follow the invoice lock instead of the issued state.
- **D-08:** Fixed, never editable: the document **number** and the document **kind** (invoice / proforma / credit note). The series stays gap-free. — **Reversibility:** one-way — a changed number would break the gap-free guarantee and every printed or sent reference.

### PDF, e-mail, payment links
- **D-09:** Saving an edit **regenerates the PDF** and replaces the stored one in `documents` (existing `RegeneratePdf` flow); the replace is not subject to the deletion confirmation of Phase 9 D-03. No PDF version history.
- **D-10:** After an edit, nothing is sent automatically. Admin re-sends manually; a successful send locks the invoice again.
- **D-11:** When amount or currency change, the Stripe payment link is invalidated (deactivated) and a new one created for the current amount; the SPAYD QR is regenerated with the PDF. A card payment already recorded keeps its record.

### Database guards and audit
- **D-12:** The DB guard triggers stay but are **conditional**: a lock column (for example `locked_at`) decides. An issued-but-unlocked invoice, its items and its linked time entries are writable; a locked one refuses INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE (also raw SQL), except what stays writable today (payment state, internal note). Number and kind have their own always-on immutability rule. Exact column and trigger design is the planner's call but must keep the raw-SQL test helper (`Immutability`) coverage. — **Reversibility:** costly — migration of the guards of `invoices`, `invoice_items`, `time_entries` link guard.
- **D-13:** Every change to an issued invoice after its first send, plus every unlock and lock, is written to the Audit domain with who, when and before/after (items, dates, parties, currency) and, for an unlock, the reason. Invoices that were never sent are not logged (they behave like drafts). **Owner ruling (2026-10-23), amends D-13 for the bank account:** a change of the bank account is audited by its non-reversible keyed fingerprint (`bank_fingerprint`, the fingerprint before and after, with who and when), never by the account values, so the account number never enters the history (T-10.1-18).

### Claude's Discretion
- Names (action classes, lock column, enum/state), UI placement of the Unlock action and warnings, wording of cs/en strings, and how the Partner view shows an edited invoice (it follows existing isolation rules and sees only the current version).

</decisions>
