# Phase 10.1 Plan 14: Bank refill audit by keyed fingerprint Summary

**A post-send bank refill now leaves one `invoice_edited` row with who, when and the HMAC-SHA256 fingerprint of the account before and after, and the account itself never enters the history.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 6 min (2026-10-23T06:41:44Z to 06:47:54Z)
- **Tasks:** 2 (1 tracer, 1 tdd auto)
- **Files modified:** 3

## Accomplishments

- Gap 2 of 10.1-VERIFICATION.md (review WR-02) closed: changing the account printed on a sent invoice is audited.
- `InvoiceAudit::snapshot()` carries `bank_fingerprint`; `edited()` diffs it like any other key, so no change to `UpdateIssuedDocument` was needed.
- The key reads `Bankovní účet (otisk)` in the history tab through the existing plain label lookup.
- Hardening: recursive key sort (mutation check: without `ksort` four tests fail, so jsonb key order is a real hazard), null/empty snapshot gives null, same-account refill writes nothing.

## Owner decision

The bank account is audited by fingerprint, not by value. Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-23, reason T-10.1-18 (the account number stays out of the history on purpose). This narrows the "before and after" of D-13 and IN-15 for this one field only. IN-15 (`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`) and D-13 (`10.1-CONTEXT.md`) are amended by plan 10.1-16, not by this plan; neither file was edited here.
