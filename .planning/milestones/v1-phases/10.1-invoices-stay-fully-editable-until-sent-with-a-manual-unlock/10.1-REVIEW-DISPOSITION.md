# Phase 10.1 review disposition

Source: 10.1-REVIEW.md (1 critical, 4 warning, 6 info). The findings were triaged in the gap-closure round (plans 10.1-13 to 10.1-16, 2026-10-23): three are fixed with a test and a commit, eight are deferred with a reason. Nothing is left without a disposition.

| CR-01 | critical | fixed | Plan 10.1-13. `RecordCardPayment` step 5 now records unmatched `currency` unless the paid currency, the link's currency and the document's current currency are one. The database backs it: migration `2026_10_23_000100_pin_card_payment_currency_to_its_payment.php` adds `kokpit_guard_card_payment_currency()` and the trigger `card_payments_currency_guard` (KP001). Tests: the two webhook tests of `PaymentClassificationTest.php` (former-currency link unmatched with no payment and no income; the new link still matched) and the raw-SQL block of `CardPaymentRecordTest.php`. Commits `d4a7ab3`, `c3e0266`. |
| WR-01 | warning | fixed | Plan 10.1-15. `InvoiceEmail::staleBefore()` is the one stale cutoff; `IssuedDocumentRules::emailInFlight()` ages a queued row like a sending row, so a lost job no longer blocks an edit; `SendInvoiceEmail` claims a row under the invoice row lock and gives a stale queued row up as failed (class `stale`, one bell notice) instead of sending it late; the Odeslani tab reads such a row as failed and offers "Odeslat znovu". | WR-02 | warning | fixed | Plan 10.1-14. `InvoiceAudit::snapshot()` carries `bank_fingerprint` (12 hex characters of an HMAC keyed with the app key over the key-sorted bank snapshot), so a bank-only refill after the first send writes one `invoice_edited` row with the fingerprints before and after and no account data; a refill that brings back the same account writes nothing. Owner decision of 2026-10-23 (reason T-10.1-18): fingerprint instead of values; IN-15 and D-13 amended by plan 10.1-16. Tests: five in `InvoiceEditAuditTest.php`. Commits `e285347`, `71f3e5a`. |
| WR-03 | warning | deferred | Out of scope of this round. Database protection needs an owner decision: lock the document at storno and at conversion, or extend the guard predicates of `kokpit_guard_invoice_row()` and the child guard. |
| WR-04 | warning | deferred | Needs an owner decision. The document e-mail is sent before the lock transaction, and a failure of the lock or audit step puts the row back to queued, so a retry mails the customer again. Every fix chooses a degraded state for "transport accepted, lock step failed" and touches a phase decision (D-01 a delivered document is locked; the 10.1-01 ruling that the sent mark and the lock share one transaction; the documented at-least-once guarantee T-10-64). Options: **A** keep the at-least-once resend (today's behaviour); **B** never resend after the transport accepted: the row stays sending, the exception is reported and the Admins are told the document was sent but not locked, the document stays open until the Admin acts; **C** persist the delivery first (a delivered marker or a new e-mail state, needs a migration of `invoice_emails_sent_at_check` and of the state CHECK and labels), then retry only the lock and audit step without resending, edits keep waiting while a delivered row is not locked, a permanent failure still needs an Admin decision. Recommendation of the planner: C as a bounded follow-up plan of about 2 tasks; the choice is the owner's. |
