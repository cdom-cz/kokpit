# Phase 10.1 Plan 04: The domain write path of an open issued document Summary

**An open issued invoice or proforma can have its lines and printed note corrected in one transaction: amounts are recomputed on the server, a converted time line keeps its frozen exchange rate, the stored PDF is replaced and nothing is sent.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 60 min
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 10

## Accomplishments

- `UpdateIssuedDocument` (R-10.1-04-3): authorises `update`, locks the invoice row, asks `IssuedDocumentRules::assertEditable()` on the locked row, writes the lines through `ItemListWriter` with the issued pricing and the credited-line rule, sums the totals (`total_not_positive` otherwise), saves with `forceFill` (totals) and `fill` (note), and ends with `IssuedEditFinisher::finish()`. It never reads or writes the number, kind, state, lock, snapshots or card switch.
- `ItemListWriter` (R-10.1-04-1): the bodies of `saveItems`, `assertOwnLines`, the parking with `PARKED_POSITION`, `writeLine`, `isGenerated` and `totals` extracted from `UpdateDraft`; the draft tests passed unchanged (101 tests across the four draft files).
- `IssuedDocumentRules` and `Invoice::isEditable()` (R-10.1-04-2): `editBlocker()` returns `not_issued`, `credit_note_locked`, `document_locked`, `cancelled_not_editable`, `converted_not_editable` or null; `assertEditable()` throws the `document` error.
- `ReplaceIssuedPdf` (R-10.1-04-5): renders first, locks the Admin-only row, deletes the old PDF through `DeleteDocument` with `confirmedIssuedPdf: true`, stores and links the new one; a render or storage failure becomes the fixed `regenerate_failed` message and rolls the save back.
- `IssuedEditFinisher` (R-10.1-04-4): recomputes `czk_total_minor` from the stored trio exactly as `DocumentIssuer::writeAdminRow()` does, then replaces the PDF; the docblock keeps the slots for the card link step (plan 10.1-05) and the audit step (plan 10.1-12).
- `TimeLineConversion::fromSnapshot()` (R-10.1-04-6): `convertThroughCzk()` and `timesThroughCzk()` with the quotes of the conversion snapshot, one rounding per line.
- Czech copy (R-10.1-04-9): `not_issued`, `document_locked`, `email_in_flight`, `credited_line_frozen`, `credited_line_delete`, and the `invoices.warnings` pair first used by plan 10.1-12.
- `UpdateIssuedDocumentTest.php`: 22 tests covering the tracer, all five refusals with their copy, the open proforma, partial payment and partial credit note as non-blockers, foreign and duplicate ids, the non-positive total, the three frozen fields and the delete of a credited line, and the frozen EUR conversion against a later CNB list.

## Decisions Made

- Followed rulings R-10.1-04-1 to R-10.1-04-6 and R-10.1-04-9 as written; R-10.1-04-7 and R-10.1-04-8 are plan 10.1-12.
