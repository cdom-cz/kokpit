# Phase 10.1 Plan 07: Header of an open issued document Summary

**An open issued invoice or proforma now has its issue and due dates, language, customer and supplier lines and bank account editable in the one editor, with the issue date held inside its numbering period and between its series neighbours, the frozen CNB rate kept, and every changed field audited after the first send.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 70 min
- **Tasks:** 3 (one tracer, two auto)
- **Files modified:** 7 (2 new)

## Accomplishments

- `UpdateIssuedDocument` takes the header keys of R-10.1-07-1. Only keys that are present and differ from the stored value are written (`forceFill`); the number, kind, VAT mode and appearance are never among them. Dates go through `PragueDay` and are never null; the due date is checked against the effective issue date whenever either moved; `language` through `InvoiceLanguage`.
- `IssuedDocumentRules::assertIssueDate()` (R-10.1-07-2): not after today (Prague), the same allocator scope key at Prague noon of the old and the new day under the pattern the series has now, and between the issue dates of the previous and next issued document of the same kind ordered by `issued_at`, bounds included. All problems are returned together under `issue_date`. `keepsIssueRates()` tells the editor when the "rates stay" warning applies (document not in CZK, or a line with a conversion snapshot).
- `SnapshotInput` (R-10.1-07-3): customer lines follow the client columns, supplier lines follow `SupplierSettings::rules()` (`company_name` is accepted for `name`), trimmed, empty to null, unknown keys ignored, an absent field keeps its stored value, only changed fields are checked, `vat_payer` is copied from the stored snapshot. Errors are keyed `customer.<field>` and `supplier.<field>`.
- `reload_bank` (strict boolean) takes the bank snapshot from `InvoiceSnapshot::bank($currency)`; `bank_missing` under `bank` when there is none. A `bank_snapshot` in the data is ignored.
- Editor: issue date, due date and language are editable (currency stays read-only for plan 10.1-08); a section "Odběratel a dodavatel" with two fieldsets, refill actions "Načíst z karty klienta" and "Načíst z nastavení dodavatele" that change the form only, the bank account shown read-only with "Načíst bankovní účet z nastavení"; after a save that moved the issue date of a document with kept rates a persistent warning is sent; the taxable supply date stays hidden in the non-payer mode.
- Tests: new `IssuedHeaderEditTest.php` (42 cases with datasets) and two editor cases in `IssuedEditorTest.php`.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Plan inconsistency] A customer name of 201 characters is not an error**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (writing the behavior tests)
- **Issue:** the behavior list says a customer name of 201 characters is a field error, but R-10.1-07-3 sets the customer limits to those of the client columns (255). The 200 limit belongs to the supplier name (`SupplierSettings`).
- **Fix:** kept the ruling (255), so a name copied from the client card always fits; the tests use 256 characters for the customer and 201 for the supplier name.
- **Files modified:** `tests/Feature/Invoicing/IssuedHeaderEditTest.php`
- **Commit:** f274017, faf37fc

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing copy] One message cannot name a missing neighbour**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** R-10.1-07-2 gives one `issue_date_order` text with both dates, but a first or last document of the series has only one neighbour.
- **Fix:** `issue_date_order` is used when both neighbours exist; `issue_date_after_previous` and `issue_date_before_next` carry the single bound otherwise.
- **Files modified:** `lang/cs/invoices.php`, `IssuedDocumentRules.php`
- **Commit:** 0ec4ad4

**3. [Rule 2 - Missing handling] Errors with no form field**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** `bank` (and `document`) have no field in the form, so a `data.bank` error would be invisible.
- **Fix:** the editor shows `bank_missing` as a danger notification and halts, and the refill button refuses a currency without an account before the flag is set.
- **Files modified:** `EditInvoice.php`
- **Commit:** faf37fc

**Total deviations:** 3 auto-fixed (1 Rule 1, 2 Rule 2). **Impact:** none on scope; two extra copy keys and one extra check.
