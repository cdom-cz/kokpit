Decisions made in this plan (recorded):
- Amounts are stored positive and the type gives the direction (research A14); `czk_minor` is `>= 0` because a tiny foreign amount can round to zero.
- `transactions.type` joins the composite FK `(category_id, type)`, so it gets a `PgSchema::EXEMPT` entry with a reason.
- In this plan `czk_source` is a plain string attribute (the CHECK holds the three values); plan 09-22 adds the `CzkSource` enum with its labels, the cast and `czk()`. `lang/cs/enums.php` is therefore not touched here, which keeps this plan free of shared files with plan 09-05 in wave 3.
- The canary fixture writes its row with `forceFill` as system (identity CZK amount), not through an Action; the write Actions are plan 09-22.
- Activity logging of transactions is added in plan 09-11 together with the screen (D-14).
- Assumption-delta checkpoint (advisory; detector fired on "optional client and project"): primary noun = the transaction; decision `no-change` - the client and project are optional attributes of a transaction, not its anchor; the anchor (date, type, category, amount) stays required.
- Revision at plan check (scope): the former 16-file plan was split; this plan keeps the table, model and registrations (which must land together, the arch and canary suites enforce it) and moved one wave earlier (it no longer shares `lang/cs/enums.php` with plan 09-05 or needs the finance copy of plan 09-06); the CZK rule, `TransactionInput` and the write Actions moved to plan 09-22.
