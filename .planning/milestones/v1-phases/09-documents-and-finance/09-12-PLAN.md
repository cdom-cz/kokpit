Decisions carried in (locked): D-02 as amended by D-22 (attachments of comments are uploaded directly in the comment form through a separate field; the rich editors keep `fileAttachments(false)` by design, so uploads cannot bypass the policy and the sanitiser), D-04 (never an internal-comment attachment), D-05 (Partner uploads in own non-internal comments; Admin bell), D-19 (task open = not archived and status not `done`), D-20 (non-internal comment files visible by default, internal ones always hidden), Phase 5 TA-04 and A10.

Decisions made in this plan (recorded):
- `RichEditor::fileAttachments(false)` stays everywhere (D-22): inline editor files would bypass `documents`, the policy and the sanitiser (research Anti-Patterns); "replacing the markers" means the A10 comments now name the separate `files` field. The escalation and description editors of the Partner task page get no upload (research A3: two Partner surfaces only).
- Tests put files into the comment form one at a time (`commentData.files.<key>` per file, or Filament's form filling), like the browser; the suite runs the production temporary-disk setting of plan 09-24, under which Livewire refuses a list of files in one call.
- The thread view never renders a signed URL; buttons call `downloadCommentDocument(id)` / `previewCommentDocument(id)` / `deleteCommentDocument(id)` on the component, which validate the UUID, re-read the document through the scoped query restricted to comments of this task, authorize and redirect or delete.
- Comment files are eager-loaded on the comments through the scoped `documents` relation, so a Partner's query never loads an internal comment or a hidden file.
