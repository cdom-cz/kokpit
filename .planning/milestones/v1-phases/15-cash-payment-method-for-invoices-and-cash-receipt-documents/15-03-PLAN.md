<objective>
Extract the hardened invoice rendering into two shared pieces before the cash receipt needs them: the closed Dompdf engine and the PDF parts (party block, footer, image, text). The invoice PDF stays byte-for-byte the same in text; the closed options are pinned by a test of their own.

Purpose: P15-08 (the receipt PDF of 15-09 reuses the hardened renderer instead of copying it, research "Don't Hand-Roll"); D-11 (the receipt PDF is generated like the invoice PDF).
Output: `ClosedDompdf`, `PdfParts`, `InvoicePdf` and `InvoicePdfData` delegating to them, `PdfEngineTest`.
</objective>
