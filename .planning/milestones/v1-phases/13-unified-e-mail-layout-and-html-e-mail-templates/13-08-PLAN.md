- A-13-7 (research open question 6): the Platby tab shows the confirmation state per payment, so a failed or skipped confirmation is visible where the payment is. To reverse: remove the column.
- A-13-1 and A-13-6 from plan 13-06 are proven here (proformas confirmed, carried copies not; the five-minute grace window).
