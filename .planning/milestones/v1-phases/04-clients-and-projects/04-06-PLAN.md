<objective>
Store everything a Partner must never read about a project in the Admin-only 1:1 `project_billing` table and put project creation behind the `CreateProject` domain Action, which enforces the money, key, client and archive rules in one transaction.

Purpose: PR-03 ("rates and prices live where Partner cannot read them") with D-05's separate table, and the creation half of PR-01 and PR-02 so the Admin form (plan 04-08) is a thin adapter. Phase 10 billing reads rates, fixed prices and estimates from here. The update path, the estimate conversion and auditing follow in plan 04-07.
Output: the migration, `ProjectBilling`, `BillingType`, `CreateProject`, the canary fixture and the creation tests.
</objective>

Assumptions adopted from research (unattended run, recorded and easy to reverse):
- Research item 2 (Open Questions 2 and 3, A4): `billing_type` and a new Admin-only `internal_note` live in `project_billing`, so a Partner query never loads them (D-07 names billing type and internal notes as hidden).
- Research item 3 (A3): the project half (a project's client is immutable after creation) lands with `UpdateProject` in plan 04-07; the client-currency lock half lands with `UpdateClient` in plan 04-11, using `ProjectBilling::clientHoldsMoney()` from plan 04-07.
- Research Pattern 6 optional CHECK adopted: a fixed price is required when the billing type is `fixed_price` (`project_billing_fixed_price_required_check`). Reversal is one migration that drops the constraint.
- Own decision (reason): the estimate is stored as `estimate_seconds`, not minutes, because whole seconds are the project's duration unit (STATE.md, Phase 2 Money decision); the form enters hours with at most two decimals, which convert to whole seconds without rounding (`EstimateHours`, plan 04-07).
- Cross-table currency equality (project money currency equals the client currency) is enforced by the Actions and by the client-currency lock, not by a trigger.

Fictional data only; `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
