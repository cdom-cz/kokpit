Assumptions adopted from research (unattended run, recorded and easy to reverse):
- Research item 4 (Open Question 5, A7): "exactly one primary contact" is enforced by the partial unique index (at most one) plus the Action layer (first contact becomes primary, the primary cannot be deleted while others exist, the switch demotes before it promotes). No deferred constraint trigger: the Actions are the only write path (the relation manager never binds `is_primary` to a form field), and a trigger can be added by one migration later.
- Contacts have no soft delete (plain data; later invoices snapshot the recipient; the activity log records the delete). Contacts are not a repeater, because a repeater rewrites the whole collection and fights the partial unique index (research anti-pattern).

Fictional data only: `Example contact` names, `exampleEmail()` addresses, phone numbers assembled at runtime from a fictional prefix. `scripts/check-sensitive.sh` before every commit; never bypass the hook.
</context>
