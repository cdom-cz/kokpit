# Phase 17.1: Close gap G1/G2 Verification Report

**Phase Goal:** No tracked time stays locked on an invoice that is credited in full, and no payment exists without its income. Once partial credit notes have taken back every creditable quantity, the credit note that took back the last quantity returns all its time entries to unbilled and unlocked in the same locked transaction (the invoice is not cancelled, `cancels_invoice` stays the one storno marker, the database link guard accepts exactly this release). A payment on a proforma that already has its invoice is refused by the shared `RecordPayment::refuses()` on the manual and the card path with its own message (audit gaps G1 and G2; time held by an unpaid proforma stays deferred).
