# Phase 17.1 Plan 02: Refuse payments on a converted proforma Summary

A payment on a proforma that already has its invoice is refused by the shared `RecordPayment::refuses()` rule on the manual and card paths, with a Czech message that names the invoice, and nothing is stored.

## What was built

- `RecordPayment::refuses()` now returns `?ValidationException`. The unchanged condition (not issued, no payment carried, cancelled) still gives `payment_not_allowed`. A proforma that an invoice names through `invoiceFromProforma()` gives the new `payment_on_converted_proforma` with the number of that invoice. Both `handle()` (before `PaymentInput`) and `recordLocked()` (before any write) throw what it returns. The class, `recordLocked()` and `@throws` docblocks name the new key.
- `lang/cs/invoices.php`: `payment_on_converted_proforma` in the Payments group.
- Tests: the dialog of a converted proforma that owes its total again (PaymentModalTest), the Action in both states plus the two still-allowed cases (PaymentsTest), the job path without a user (CardPaymentCoreTest), and `AssignCardPayment` of a duplicate (CardPaymentResolutionTest).
- `RecordCardPayment`, `AssignCardPayment`, `RecordPaymentAction` and `ViewCardPayment` are unchanged (D-07). No data migration (D-08).
